The International English Fluency Course

One structured pathway from a first sentence to executive-level command of English — six levels, twenty-four months, seven hundred and twenty learning units.

Academic Progression

Six levels, mapped to the CEFR.

Each level is designed to build toward its corresponding Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) band — the benchmark most widely recognised by universities, employers and English-language institutions worldwide.

LevelCEFRFocusDurationUnits
I · Foundation ProgrammeA1 First words to simple, everyday exchanges — sound system, core grammar, survival vocabulary. 4 months120
II · Elementary ProgrammeA2 Everyday topics with growing confidence — routine conversation, simple written communication. 4 months120
III · Intermediate ProgrammeB1 Independent use of English — connected speech, opinions, and structured writing. 4 months120
IV · Upper Intermediate ProgrammeB2 Fluent, spontaneous interaction — academic and professional register, argument and analysis. 4 months120
V · Advanced ProgrammeC1 Precise, flexible use of English for complex academic and professional purposes. 4 months120
VI · English Mastery ProgrammeC2 Near-native command — nuance, idiom, leadership communication and executive presence. 4 months120

Assessment & Certification

Progress that's measured, not assumed.

Weekly quizzes and monthly assessments track progress within each level; a mid-level and a final examination confirm readiness to advance. Every level closes with a capstone project applying that level's skills to a real task.

  • Digital transcript updated after every level
  • Digital certificate on successful completion of the full programme
  • IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge English preparation embedded from the Upper Intermediate level onward
  • Continuous progress monitoring visible to students at every stage
