Faculty

Instructors held to a professional standard, not a script.

WEC-LC's teaching approach is built around expert, credentialed instructors delivering live and recorded instruction across all six levels of the IEFC.

Teaching Philosophy

Expert instruction, measured outcomes.

Structured, Not Improvised

Every class follows the IEFC's mapped curriculum — instructors teach to a shared standard across every level and every cohort.

Speaking-First

Guided speaking practice and live conversation are built into every level, not treated as an optional extra.

Accountable to Data

Weekly quizzes, monthly assessments and continuous progress monitoring mean instruction adjusts to how each cohort is actually performing.

What We Look For

Our bar for joining the founding faculty.

Every instructor we appoint is measured against the same standard, regardless of where in the world they teach from.

Certification & Fluency

  • A recognised English-teaching certification (CELTA, DELTA, TESOL, or equivalent) or a degree in English, Linguistics, or Education
  • Native or C2-level (CEFR Mastery) fluency in English

Experience & Fit

  • Demonstrated experience teaching to a structured, level-based curriculum — CEFR-aligned experience is a strong preference
  • Comfortable and effective teaching live, on video, in a fully online classroom
  • Alignment with WEC-LC's speaking-first, data-accountable teaching methodology (see Academics · Teaching Methodology)
