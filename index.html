Empowering the World Through English Excellence.

WorldWide English College, London Campus (WEC-LC) is an international English language institution built around one pathway — the International English Fluency Course (IEFC) — carrying learners from their first word to mastery. The whole programme is written, published and open to inspection before anyone pays for it.

01 · What Makes This Different

You can read the entire programme before you pay for it.

Most language providers ask you to buy a syllabus you have never seen. Every level here is published: its modules, its outcomes, its assessments and the criteria they are marked against.

One

The standard is published first

Outcomes, rubrics and pass criteria reach you before the assessment, not afterwards. A standard revealed after the fact is not a standard — it is an explanation of a verdict.

Two

Four skills, marked apart

Listening, reading, speaking and writing are marked separately and stay separate on your record. One averaged number hides exactly what a language learner most needs to know.

Three

What is missing is written down

Where the College lacks something — an external examiner, appointed board members, classroom evidence — it says so on the page where it matters, not once in a footnote.

02 · The Programme

The International English Fluency Course

Total qualification time is a design figure, not a measurement: it is the workload the curriculum was built to, and it has not yet been measured against real learners because there have not been any. How many months that becomes depends entirely on the hours you can give it each week, which is why the College publishes hours rather than a number of months it could not stand behind. The WEC Credit is an internal measure and is not an ECTS or CATS credit.

04 · The Digital Campus

What is built, and what has not yet run.

The platform is finished and tested. Some of it has never been used, because no cohort has been taught — and a homepage that blurred those two things would be the least trustworthy sentence on this site.

Built

My Programme

Your level, module by module, with what is complete and what is next. Progression is per learner — nothing unlocks on a date.

Built

The Listening Lab

You record yourself against named pronunciation targets, and the recordings are kept so six months of change is audible rather than assumed. It records offline when a connection drops.

Built

Your Academic Record

Every attempt, mark and piece of feedback, visible as it accumulates. You decide what is shared and with whom.

Not yet

Live classes and recorded audio

The listening scripts are written; the audio has not been recorded. No live session has run, because no cohort has been taught. Both are stated rather than implied.

06 · Before You Apply

Four things this College will tell you that others would not.

No

Accreditation

WEC-LC holds no accreditation and no external quality-assurance affiliation. This is stated wherever it bears on your decision, and it will read differently only when it genuinely is.

No

Conferred award

No External Examiner has been appointed, so no award has been conferred on anyone. Conferring one without external examining would make it worth less, not more.

No

Graduates, or graduate statistics

No cohort has been taught. The College publishes no completion rates, employment outcomes or student numbers, because it has none.

No

Refund policy

None has been adopted. Requests are decided case by case in writing. If certainty matters to you, pay level by level rather than in full — see Refunds.

Institutional Status

WEC-LC is a newly established institution, delivering the IEFC online worldwide from a London administrative headquarters — an administrative address, not a teaching campus. The programme structure, curriculum, assessment scheme and tuition on this site are confirmed. Accreditation, a registered HQ address, the academic calendar and appointments to the two academic bodies are outstanding, and each is listed as outstanding rather than described as in progress. The full picture is at About · Institutional Status, and the evidence behind it at the evidence record.
