Where to begin

Six questions, and a reading

Not a quiz, and nothing is scored. Answer as much as you care to and the College will set out which routes are open to you, why each one, and what each leads to afterwards. Where an answer touches a limit — a pace below what the Regulations require, a faculty that is not yet open — it will say so rather than route around it.

Nothing you type here leaves your browser. No answer is sent, stored or counted, and no account is asked for.

The reading

Answer three or more and it will appear here. The first route named is where the College would ordinarily begin you; the others are open to you too, and the reason for each is given.
