Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the IEFC programme, admissions, and studying with WEC-LC.

The IEFC is WEC-LC's flagship 24-month English programme, structured as six CEFR-aligned levels (A1 through C2), each four months long, carrying 20 WEC Credits and a Total Qualification Time of 200 hours. The full programme is 120 WEC Credits and 1,200 hours.
Yes. WEC-LC delivers the IEFC through a digital campus — HD video lectures, live online classes, and an interactive learning portal — so students anywhere in the world can enrol without relocating.
No. Every applicant completes a short placement assessment during admissions, and is placed at the level that matches their current English ability — from Foundation through Advanced.
The full six-level programme is $19,000, or $3,166.67 per level. You can pay in full, per level, or — subject to institutional policy — in monthly instalments. See Tuition & Fees for the full breakdown.
Yes. IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge English preparation are built into the curriculum, particularly from the Upper Intermediate level onward, alongside academic and business English.
School pupils, secondary and university students, working professionals, government employees, business executives, and international students preparing for study abroad or advanced English proficiency.
A digital transcript is issued after every level, and a digital certificate is issued on successful completion of the full IEFC. Printed certificates and certificate verification are available as optional add-on services.
WEC-LC is a newly established institution. Formal accreditation and external quality-assurance affiliations are in progress and will be published on our Institutional Status page as they're confirmed — we would rather tell you the current state plainly than overstate it.
Complete the online application on the Admissions page with your name, email and country of residence — a short self-assessment first suggests your likely starting level. See Admissions for the full five-step journey.
