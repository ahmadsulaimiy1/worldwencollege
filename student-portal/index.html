Student Portal

Your entire academic life, in one digital campus.

The WEC-LC Student Portal is the home base for every IEFC learner — classes, assignments, progress, and certification, accessible from anywhere.

What's Inside

Built around the student, level by level.

01

Dashboard

A live view of your current level, upcoming classes and outstanding assignments.

02

Live & Recorded Classes

Join live sessions or catch up on HD recordings on your own schedule.

03

Assignments & Exams

Submit assignments and sit online examinations, with automatic grading where appropriate.

04

Attendance & Analytics

Track attendance and see your progress analytics update as you move through each unit.

05

Digital Library

Reading, listening and reference resources for your current level.

06

Transcripts & Certificates

A digital transcript after every level, and a digital certificate on completion.

07

Notifications

Stay on top of deadlines, results and announcements without checking in constantly.

08

Mobile-Friendly Access

The same portal, designed to work cleanly on a phone or tablet.
