# Audit 08 — An external examiner

**Who I am.** I have been an external examiner for eleven years across
three institutions. I have been asked whether I would take the
appointment here. I read the material as I would read a first-year
briefing pack.

---

## Can I do the job at all?

The question an examiner asks before any other is: **will I be able to
see what I need to see, and will anyone stop me?**

Here, yes, and unusually so.

- **Marks are traceable.** A first-marker mark and a moderated mark are
  recorded distinctly. In two of the three institutions where I have
  served, I had to ask for that and was told the system could not
  produce it.
- **The moderator cannot be the first marker.** The record refuses it.
  Not a policy — a constraint.
- **A pass list requires a countersignature from somebody other than the
  approver.** Also enforced by the record.
- **My own conflicts must be declared and cannot be left blank.** The
  register will not accept an examiner row without them.
- **My report cannot be filed as anything other than "standards met"
  without an institutional response attached.** So the College cannot
  receive a critical report and simply hold it.

That last one is the item I would normally have to negotiate. It is
already built.

---

## What I would report in my first year

### 1. There is nothing to examine yet

No cohort has completed a level. No marks exist. My first report would
be a report on readiness, not on standards.

### 2. Learning outcomes are `interim` and should not stay so

Four outcomes per level, sensibly written, stated before the teaching
was designed — which is the right order and rarer than it should be.
They are flagged `interim` because no ratifying body has met.

The Academic Senate is established and has never convened. Ratifying the
outcomes is its first job and nothing else can properly precede it.

### 3. The competency framework is not in use

Six competencies, 0 of 120 assessments mapped. The College says so.

I would not press for rapid mapping. Mapping an assessment to a
competency is an academic judgement and doing it quickly would be worse
than not doing it. I would press for **a date and a named body**, and
BASCE — which exists with zero members appointed — is that body.

### 4. Speaking is excluded from certification

Adopted deliberately, because no moderated marking standard for speech
exists. Correct, and I would have insisted on it if it had not been
done.

I would make it my first standing recommendation: **build the speaking
moderation standard.** Until it exists, a fluency qualification is
certified on everything except fluency, and no amount of candour about
that makes it comfortable.

### 5. The thresholds question is the real academic work

Five attainment bands are named. What evidence earns *Proficient* rather
than *Developing* has not been determined for any skill.

This is the hardest question in the whole architecture and it has been
correctly identified as such and correctly reserved. It is also
completely blocking: no descriptor can be reported for any graduate
until it is answered.

---

## Would I take the appointment?

**Yes.**

Three reasons. The records are in a state where examination is actually
possible, which is not usually true on arrival. The institution has
built the constraints that make my independence real before appointing
anybody to be independent — I have never seen that sequence. And it has
published the list of what it cannot evidence rather than waiting for me
to find it.

I would want two things in the appointment letter: a term long enough to
see a cohort through at least one full level, and an explicit
acknowledgement that my first report will say the standard cannot yet be
judged. An examiner pressed to certify a standard in year one is not an
examiner.

---

## The warning I would give the Board

You have spent a long time building the apparatus of external scrutiny
and no time obtaining any.

Every academic item on your outstanding list — outcomes ratification,
competency mapping, skill thresholds, conferral of any award at all —
is downstream of appointing me or somebody like me. The apparatus does
not improve while it waits. It just gets older, and the day somebody
asks how long you have been "preparing for external review", the answer
starts to be the finding.
