# Audit 07 — A university considering a progression agreement

**Who I am.** International partnerships office, a mid-ranking UK
university. A regional agent has proposed Worldwide English College as a
feeder for our pre-master's. I assess whether we could sign an
articulation agreement — a stated English level that guarantees entry.

---

## What an articulation agreement requires

1. A legal counterparty we can contract with.
2. A qualification with a known standard.
3. External validation of that standard by somebody other than the
   provider.
4. A cohort history showing that its graduates perform as predicted.
5. A dispute and termination mechanism.

The College currently satisfies none of the five. I want to be careful
about *why*, because the reasons differ in kind.

---

## Item by item

**1. Counterparty — absent.** No registered entity is published. Our
legal team contracts with companies, not with domains.

**2. Known standard — designed, unmoderated.** The CEFR mapping is
declared level by level, the outcomes are published, the rubrics are
published. This is more than most feeders give us. But the standard has
never been tested against an external reference point and the College
does not claim it has.

**3. External validation — none, and none sought.** No accreditation, no
External Examiner, no external academic review. This is the item that
makes an agreement impossible rather than merely premature.

**4. Cohort history — none.** Zero graduates. We would be guaranteeing
entry to the output of a process that has never run.

**5. Dispute mechanism — partial.** There is an academic appeals
procedure drafted, and it is explicitly *not adopted*. There is no
complaints route that is not an academic appeal.

---

## What I want to record in the College's favour

Three things, because a rejection that lists only failures is a
dishonest rejection.

**The CEFR mapping is declared, not implied.** Most feeders tell us "our
Advanced course is roughly B2". This College names the band per level in
a published table and marks its own learning outcomes `interim` because
they have not been ratified. When we eventually benchmark them, we will
be benchmarking a stated claim rather than reverse-engineering one.

**The academic record is designed for us.** Separate skill marks, marker
identity, moderated versus first mark held distinctly, an assessment
history rather than a final grade. If we ever did partner, the data we
would need to monitor the agreement already exists in the shape we would
ask for. That almost never happens.

**It does not pretend.** The site tells me it is not accredited, has no
examiner and has produced no graduates, without my having to find out. I
have terminated agreements with providers who concealed less than that.

---

## Verdict

**No agreement is possible now. A clear path exists to one.**

I would revisit if and when: a legal entity exists; an External Examiner
has produced at least one annual report; and a first cohort has
completed at least one level with results we can see.

At that point the conversation would be a real one, and it would start
further along than most. The unusual thing about this College is that
the *absent* items are all administrative and the *present* items are
all academic. Normally it is the reverse, and the reverse is much harder
to fix.

---

## What I would tell the agent who proposed it

Come back in a year with an examiner's report. Do not come back with a
prospectus.
