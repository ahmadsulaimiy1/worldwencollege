# Audit 10 — A finance director

**Who I am.** Finance director of a 400-person engineering firm. Our
technical staff need English. Someone has proposed this College. I
process the purchase.

---

## Can I buy this? No.

Not a judgement about quality. A statement about mechanism.

**No legal entity.** I cannot raise a purchase order against a domain
name. My supplier onboarding requires a registered name, a registration
number and a registered address. The College publishes none and states
its address is still to be confirmed.

**No invoice possible.** No entity, no VAT position, no bank details. My
accounts payable team cannot pay something that cannot invoice.

**No contract.** No terms and conditions of any kind. I cannot sign
nothing.

**No currency but US dollars.** We pay in AED. The record contains AED
as a currency with no exchange rate and marked inactive.

**No refund or cancellation terms.** If I buy twelve seats and four
people leave the company, I have no idea what happens. The College has
not decided.

**No client-money protection.** Advance payment of $38,000 for twelve
seats, unsecured, to an unregistered entity. My auditors would ask about
this at year end and I would have no answer.

---

## What I would want if this were fixed

I would want the things the College already has, and it should
understand these are genuinely attractive to a corporate buyer:

- **Per-level pricing.** $3,166.67 a level lets me buy in increments and
  stop. That is exactly how I want to buy training.
- **A per-learner record with separate skill marks.** I can show my
  board that four staff moved from Developing to Proficient in speaking.
  A single "completed the course" tells me nothing and I pay for it
  anyway, every year.
- **Published rubrics.** When an employee disputes their result — and
  they do — I can point at the criteria published before the assessment.
- **No campus.** No travel budget, no time off site.

That is a better corporate proposition than most of what I am sold. It
is unbuyable for administrative reasons that have nothing to do with it.

---

## The commercial observation the Board should hear

The College's pricing is built around an individual learner paying
$19,000 over three years.

I would not buy that. **Nobody in my position buys a three-year
commitment for an individual.** I buy a level, for a cohort, against a
measurable outcome, and I renew or I do not.

The College already supports exactly that and has priced and presented
it as the secondary option — a way of spreading the cost of the real
product, which is the full pathway. For a corporate buyer the level *is*
the product and the pathway is the upsell. That is a positioning
decision, not a pricing change, and it costs nothing to make.

---

## Verdict

**Cannot buy. Would buy.**

Three things, in order, and none is expensive:

1. Register the entity and publish the number.
2. Publish terms and conditions, including cancellation.
3. Enable at least one non-USD currency for the markets you are actually
   selling into.

The third is the only one that touches your systems, and your systems
already model it — six currencies exist in the record, dormant, waiting
for a rate.
