# English for Da'wah and Islamic Communication (EDIC)
Overview:
The English for Da’wah and Islamic Communication (EDIC) programme is aimed at those who wish to master the English language to engage in Islamic propagation effectively. It is designed to strengthen one’s ability to communicate religious concepts and teachings fluently and professionally. Participants will gain proficiency in Islamic terminologies, public speaking, and writing, specifically geared towards religious and academic contexts.
Programme Sections
A. Foundational Level: Islamic Terminologies and Basic English Grammar (3 months)
Focus: This stage builds foundational English skills necessary for the effective communication of Islamic terminologies in both daily conversations and formal interactions.
Certification: Certificate of Completion in Basic Da’wah English.
B. Intermediate Level: Public Speaking for Islamic Propagation (4 months
Focus: Focusing on public speaking techniques, this level will train students to deliver Islamic messages in an impactful manner. It includes mastering voice modulation, articulation, and audience engagement.
Certification: Certificate in Public Speaking for Da’wah.
C. Advanced Level: Professional Writing and Academic Communication (5 months)
Focus: Participants will develop skills in writing professional Islamic articles, preparing sermons (Khutbah), and engaging in academic English writing specifically related to Islamic studies.
Certification: Diploma in Islamic and Professional Communication.
D. Islamic Terminology Translation Certification (ITTC)
Focus: A specialised section focusing on the correct translation of Islamic texts, particularly from Arabic to English. It covers classical texts, Islamic poetry, and the accurate usage of terminologies in both oral and written forms.
Certification: Ijazah in Islamic Terminology Translation (for advanced learners).
Key Features of the EDIC Programme:
Key Features of the EDIC Programme:
Da’wah-Centred Curriculum: Tailored syllabi for each student, ensuring mastery in both Islamic propagation and English fluency.
Public Speaking and Writing Skills: Graduates will be able to deliver speeches with confidence and write professionally in religious and academic settings.
Prestigious Certification: Internationally recognised certificates that reflect genuine academic and practical achievement.
Scholarship Opportunities: Full and partial scholarships are available for eligible students, ensuring that financial constraints do not hinder educational progress.

# Earn Professional Certifications
1. Introduction
At Imam Ahmad bin Ibrohim College, we uphold the highest standards of academic integrity and ensure that our certifications are earned through genuine effort and knowledge. We do not issue fake certificates for financial gain. Our focus is on providing opportunities for those who possess strong educational backgrounds but have been hindered by societal or systemic barriers. Many individuals have gained substantial knowledge, yet due to the limitations of their environment or lack of formal recognition, they are unable to continue their academic pursuits or advance in their careers.
For these individuals, we offer a unique opportunity to earn legitimate, world-class certifications. We understand the frustration and disappointment faced by many who, despite their abilities, have been unjustly held back by the absence of proper accreditation. Our Islamic Studies Certifications, including
- Ibtida’iyyah (Islamic Primary Certificate),
- I’dadiyyah (Islamic Junior Secondary Certificate) and
- Thanawiyyah (Islamic Senior Secondary Certificate), serve as a bridge for those who wish to continue their academic journey or pursue higher education.
To ensure fairness and qualitative integrity, we provide students with a tailored syllabus specifically designed for their selected level of study. After completing the prescribed curriculum and passing the necessary examinations, students will be awarded a prestigious and globally recognized certificate. These certificates are not only a testament to their hard work and knowledge but also serve as a tool to open doors for future opportunities, whether in higher education or professional fields.
By offering this option, we aim to empower individuals who have been overlooked or denied access to formal recognition, giving them the chance to prove their skills and continue their educational and professional aspirations with confidence.
2. General Requirements for all Certifications
Certification Guidelines for Imam Ahmad bin Ibrohim Sulaimiy College
Imam Ahmad bin Ibrohim Sulaimiy College is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of Islamic education, ethical conduct, and professional credibility. Our certification programmes are designed not only to formalize the knowledge and skills of individuals but also to ensure that these certifications are awarded only to those who adhere to the correct Islamic creed, conduct, and principles of the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.
Below are the detailed, professional guidelines governing the eligibility and requirements for obtaining certificates from Imam Ahmad bin Ibrohim Sulaimiy College, including disciplinary standards and essential prerequisites for each level of certification.
1. Adherence to the Correct Islamic Creed:
Candidates must adhere to the authentic Islamic creed (Aqidah) as practiced by Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama’ah. Anyone known to deviate from the correct creed or who is involved in actions that strongly oppose the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ will not be awarded a certificate.
This includes individuals who:
- Abuse or speak ill of the companions of the Prophet ﷺ.
- Propagate deviant sects or ideologies that go against the established teachings of Islam.
- Are involved in any form of behavior or actions that contradict clear principles of the Qur'an and Sunnah.
Requirement for Attestation of Reformation:
If an individual who has previously engaged in deviant behavior repents and wishes to obtain certification, they must provide a formal attestation letter from a notable and recognized Islamic scholar. This letter must solemnly confirm that the individual has reformed and returned to the correct practices of Islam.
Only upon receiving this attestation and verifying its authenticity will the individual be reconsidered for certification.
2. Being Free of Expulsion for Disciplinary or Academic Norms
Any candidate expelled from a Madrasah or any other formal Islamic educational institution due to disciplinary reasons will not be eligible for certification. This rule ensures that individuals do not exploit our certification as a means to bypass expulsion from other institutions.
Exceptions can only be made if it is proven beyond reasonable doubt that the expulsion was unjust, and this will require a thorough investigation conducted by our college agency.
- Disciplinary Records:
If a candidate is found to have been expelled from any educational institution, the certification process will be halted until a formal investigation is completed. If the expulsion was for disciplinary reasons related to misconduct or academic dishonesty , the candidate will be permanently ineligible for certification through our programme.
- Requirement for a Back-Up Letter:
In cases where the candidate has faced disciplinary action resulting in expulsion, a back-up letter from a notable individual or recognized authority is required. This letter must indicate a strong, personal relationship between the candidate and the individual, affirming the candidate’s character and justifying their pursuit of certification.
The back-up letter will undergo verification, and Abisulaimiy College reserves the right to reject the application if the relationship or circumstances described in the letter are found to be lacking in credibility.
3. Curriculum Completion:
Candidates must complete the prescribed curriculum for their chosen certification level. The curriculum is self-paced, allowing students to access and study the material at any time before sitting for the final assessments.
4. Written Examination: After completing the curriculum, students will sit for a written examination. The passing threshold is set at 70%, ensuring that only candidates who demonstrate a solid understanding of the material can proceed.
5. Oral Examination: An oral interview will also be conducted to evaluate the candidate’s ability to articulate their knowledge verbally. This interview ensures that candidates are not only proficient in written knowledge but also in verbal communication.
6. Purpose Statement: Each candidate must submit an official statement explaining the purpose of acquiring the certification. This statement should clearly indicate how the qualification will be used, such as for further education, employment, or community service.
7. Age Requirements:
- For Ibtida'iyyah Certificate: No age limit, typically designed for younger learners starting Islamic studies.
- For I'dadiyyah Certificate: Typically for learners aged 12 years and above.
- For Thanawiyyah Certificate: Candidates must be at least 15 years of age. This ensures that students have reached the necessary maturity and educational level for secondary Islamic studies.
8. Attestation Letter (For Thanawiyyah Certification):
Candidates for the Thanawiyyah Certificate may be required to submit an attestation letter from a notable individual or recognised authority, such as a community leader, scholar, or employer. This letter serves as a formal endorsement of the candidate’s background, verifying their identity and confirming that they meet the educational and ethical standards required for certification.
9. Written and Oral Interview Requirements:
To ensure the programme remains genuine and avoids being as a ‘shortcut’ to a certificate, candidates must:
Pass the written examination with a minimum score of 70%.
Successfully complete the oral interview, which will evaluate their practical knowledge and application of Arabic Language and Islamic studies.
10. Flexibility for Non-Privileged Individuals:
Our programme is particularly crafted to help non-privileged individuals who have acquired strong Islamic knowledge and skills through informal or non-traditional learning methods. We offer flexibility in the curriculum access and assessments to accommodate their specific circumstances, but the standards for passing remain rigorous to ensure the authenticity of the qualification.
1.Sequential Certification Pathway:
Candidates seeking to obtain higher-level certificates must follow a structured academic pathway, beginning with the Ibtida'iyyah Certificate and progressing through the I'dadiyyah Certificate before qualifying for the Thanawiyyah Certificate.
Ibtida'iyyah Certificate (Foundational):
This certificate signifies completion of foundational Islamic studies and is available to students above 10 years of age.
I'dadiyyah Certificate (Intermediate):
Before applying for the Thanawiyyah Certificate, candidates must first obtain the I'dadiyyah Certificate.
Candidates who have acquired the necessary knowledge and skills but lack formal certification may be awarded the I'dadiyyah after passing both written and oral examinations and undergoing a background verification to confirm their academic history and ethical standing.
Thanawiyyah Certificate (Advanced):
Only candidates who have successfully obtained the I'dadiyyah Certificate are eligible to apply for the Thanawiyyah Certificate.
Candidates must pass the prescribed final examinations (written and oral) with a minimum score of 70% and must meet all other programme-specific requirements.
2. Attestation and Purpose Requirement:
Candidates for the Thanawiyyah Certificate and above must submit a statement of purpose, clearly outlining their reason for seeking the certification. This statement should include whether the certificate is intended for further education in Arab countries or for professional purposes.
Additionally, in certain cases, the College may require an attestation letter from a notable individual to verify the applicant's character and purpose. This ensures that certifications are not misused and are awarded only to deserving individuals.
3. Identity Verification and Age Requirements
1. Age and Educational Prerequisites:
1. Submission of Identity Documents:
All candidates must submit a government-issued identity card as part of the application process. This requirement is particularly crucial for candidates applying for the Thanawiyyah Certificate and above.
Acceptable forms of identification include:
- National Identity Card
- Passport

# Application Process

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. What programs does Imam Ahmad bin Ibrohim Sulaimiy College offer?
Our college offers a wide range of online programs, including Islamic Studies, Qur'anic Mastery, Arabic Language, and Communication Programs. We also provide Diplomas in various fields.
2. Who is eligible to apply to the college?
Our programs are open to individuals from all parts of the world. We welcome students of diverse nationalities and backgrounds, and there are no restrictions based on nationality or generation. Applicants must meet the basic educational requirements, which vary by programme.
3. How do I apply for admission?
The application process is straightforward. Complete the online application form, submit the required documents, and follow the instructions for registration. The list of required documents varies depending on the programs you are applying for.
4. What documents are required for the application?
For the Ibtida’iya (Primary) Programme, you need to submit two passport-sized photos and your past academic testimony.
For the Ijdadiyya (Intermediate) Programme, you must submit an Ibtida’iya certificate.
For the Thanawiyya (Secondary) Programme, you must submit an Ijdadiyya certificate.
For Diploma programmes, you need to submit a Thanawiyya certificate (such as NECO, WAEC, or a recognised equivalent), along with a recommendation letter from two referees and your National Identification Number (NIN).
5. What are the language proficiency requirements?
For certain programmes, such as the intensive Arabic language courses, a basic knowledge of Arabic is required. However, we also offer programmes for beginners starting from zero proficiency.
6. Is there a registration fee?
Yes, a non-refundable registration fee is required upon submission of the application form. The amount varies by programme and will be specified during the application process.
7. What are the tuition fees for the programmes?
Tuition fees depend on the programme you select. Detailed information about fees can be found on our website under the 'Tuition and Fees' section.
8. Does the college offer scholarships?
Yes, we offer scholarships for eligible students. Scholarship availability is based on merit and financial need. Detailed information on how to apply for scholarships can be found on our website.
9. Are the programmes offered online?
Yes, all our programmes are delivered entirely online. This allows students from all over the world to access our courses. Our administrative office is located in Lagos Island, but no physical attendance is required for the programmes.
10. What is the duration of the diploma programmes?
Diploma programmes typically run for 12 to 24 weeks, depending on the student’s performance and academic progress.
11. What is the admission process timeline?
Once you submit your application, it will be reviewed, and if successful, you will receive an admission letter. In some cases, an interview may be scheduled before the final decision is made.
12. How will I know if I have been admitted?
You will receive an admission letter via email once your application has been reviewed and approved.
13. Can I apply for more than one programme at the same time?
Yes, you may apply for multiple programmes if you meet the eligibility requirements for each.
14. What qualifications will I receive upon completing a programme?
Upon successful completion of a programme, you will receive a certificate, diploma, or ijazah, depending on the course of study.
15. Are the certificates recognised?
Yes, our certificates and diplomas are recognised by relevant institutions and organisations. We ensure that our qualifications meet international standards of education.
16. Can I receive financial aid for my studies?
Yes, we offer financial aid options for students who demonstrate financial need. Detailed information about how to apply can be found on our website.
17. What are the requirements for enrolling in the diploma programmes?
For diploma programmes, you must submit a Thanawiyya (Senior Secondary Certificate) such as NECO, WAEC, or an equivalent recognised certificate. Additionally, you need two recommendation letters and your National Identification Number (NIN).
18. Will I receive study materials?
Yes, study materials will be provided for all online programmes. These materials will be accessible through our online learning platform.
19. How are the classes conducted?
Classes are conducted via virtual classrooms. You will need to meet the technical requirements (computer, internet access, etc.) to participate in these online sessions.
20. Is there an examination for each programme?
Yes, each programme includes examinations that you must pass in order to graduate and earn your certification.
21. How do I prepare for the exams?
