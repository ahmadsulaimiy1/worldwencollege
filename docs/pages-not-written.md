# The pages that were requested and not written

The Founding Editorial Office brief specified a full information
architecture — roughly 120 pages across fourteen sections — and one
governing rule:

> Never invent achievements, rankings, accreditations, partnerships,
> awards, student numbers, graduate statistics, or legal status that do
> not exist. Where something has not yet been achieved, state it
> honestly or omit it. Truth is more important than appearance.

Fifty-four pages were written. This document records the ones that were
not, why, and what would make each writable. It exists so that the gap
is a decision on the record rather than an omission nobody noticed.

**The single cause behind almost all of it:** AIPC has taught nobody.
No cohort, no student, no assessment sat, no award conferred, no
graduate. Every page below is a page that describes what happens after
that, and there is no honest version of it before.

---

## Written instead of omitted, where a reframe was truthful

Six requested pages had no honest form as asked, and a truthful page on
the same subject was written in their place. These are not
substitutions of convenience; each answers the question the original
page would have been read for.

| Requested | Written instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Academic Calendar | `/admissions/dates/` | No calendar is adopted and `/about/` lists it as outstanding. The page answers "when can I start?" — the actual question — and explains why there are no term dates. |
| Alumni | `/standards/verification/` | There are no alumni. What exists is the route by which a credential will be checked, built ahead of the first award. |
| Results / Graduation | `/students/awards/` | Nothing has been conferred. The awards, the post-nominals and the proposed honours scheme are real; results are not. |
| Research / Publications | `/standards/research/` | No research output exists. A research *agenda* does, and four answerable questions are published. |
| Student Life | `/learning/` | No student has had a life here. What a week of study consists of is describable. |
| Teacher Training | `/teaching/development/` | No teacher has been trained or observed. The record that would hold it is published, empty, with the reason. |

## Omitted entirely

### 1. History / Our Story
The College has no history. It was founded, and everything since is on
`/about/#status`. A history page would be a founding date and prose
filling the space after it.
**Writable when:** there is a first cohort, a first graduation, or any
event that happened to somebody other than the founders.

### 2. News / Press Releases / Events
There is no news. Nothing has occurred that a third party would report,
no event has been held, and no press release has been issued to anyone.
A news section seeded with "AIPC launches website" is the clearest
possible signal that an institution has nothing to report.
**Writable when:** something happens. The first appointment, the first
cohort and the first conferral are each genuinely newsworthy.

### 3. Partners / Affiliations / Accreditation
There are no partners, no affiliations and no accreditation. This is
already stated on `/about/#status`, in the FAQ, and on every page where
it bears on a reader's decision.
**Writable when:** an agreement is signed with a named body. Not before,
and never as "in progress with" a body that has not agreed.

### 4. Employers / Graduate Outcomes / Testimonials
No graduate has been employed, because no graduate exists. No student
has given a testimonial, because no student exists. Both pages are
purely a function of the same absence.
**Writable when:** there are graduates, and they consent.

### 5. Annual Reports / External Review Reports
An annual report before a first year is a form with nothing in it. No
external review has been carried out, and none can be until a reviewer
is appointed. Both are recorded in the evidence register as `scheduled`
and `governance_pending` respectively, and both appear on
`/standards/evidence/` in those states.
**Writable when:** a year has passed, and a reviewer has reported.

### 6. Community / Societies / Student Union / Chapters
There is no community. The alumni-chapter structure exists as an adopted
governance decision and as an empty table; a page describing chapters
would describe a network of nobody.
**Writable when:** there are members.

### 7. Terms of Service
Deliberately not written, and for a different reason from the rest.
Terms of service are a legal instrument that binds the College and its
students. Drafting one is a legal act, not an editorial one, and an
editorial office producing plausible-sounding terms would be creating
enforceable-looking obligations nobody with authority had approved. The
factual position — what is collected, what is charged, what is refunded,
what is not provided — is published across `/support/privacy/`,
`/admissions/payment/`, `/admissions/policy/` and `/students/support/`.
**Writable when:** a qualified person drafts it and the Executive adopts
it.

### 8. Leadership
No principal officer is appointed. A Leadership page would be either
empty or invented, and `docs/org-chart-placeholders.md` exists precisely
because inventing one was considered and refused.
`/about/structure/` names the posts and states which are vacant, which is
the honest version of the same page.
**Writable when:** somebody is appointed.

---

## What this leaves

Fifty-four written pages, every one of which rests on something that
exists: a curriculum, a platform, a published assessment scheme, a
governance register, an evidence register, twelve produced volumes, and
a named faculty.

The pages above are not missing because the work was not done. They are
missing because the events they describe have not happened, and the
alternative to omitting them was to invent them.
