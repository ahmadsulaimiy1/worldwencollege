# WEC-LC — Level I, Module 7: Past Experiences I

*Part of `docs/curriculum-level-1-foundation.md`'s Level I build-out.*

**Module learning objectives.** By the end of Module 7, the learner
can: talk about actions completed in the past using simple past
(regular and common irregular verbs); ask and answer simple past
`yes/no` and `wh-` questions; use common past-time expressions
(yesterday, last week, two days ago); narrate a short simple past
event in sequence.

**Content map (5 learning items):** module overview & key phrases;
Lesson 7.1 ("Yesterday — Regular Past Verbs"); Lesson 7.2 ("Last
Weekend — Irregular Past Verbs & Questions"); Module 7 Quiz (8
questions); Module 7 Assignment ("My Last Holiday").

---

## Reference reading: Module overview & key phrases

**Key phrases introduced this module:** Yesterday I.../Last week
I... — Did you...? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. — What did you do? —
Two days ago... — I went.../I saw.../I had...

**Key vocabulary previewed:** regular past verbs (worked, watched,
studied, played, cooked, cleaned, visited), common irregular past
verbs (went, saw, had, ate, did, made, bought), past-time expressions
(yesterday, last night/week/weekend, two days ago).

## Lesson 7.1 — "Yesterday — Regular Past Verbs"

**Learning objectives.** By the end of this lesson you can: (1) form
the regular simple past correctly (`-ed`); (2) use 8+ regular past
verbs to talk about yesterday; (3) form simple past negative
statements (`didn't + base verb`); (4) use `yesterday`/`last night`
correctly with the simple past.

**Prerequisite knowledge.** Module 3 (present simple, as the contrast
point for this new tense).

**Warm-up (5 min).** Instructor mimes 3-4 yesterday activities
("cooked", "cleaned", "watched TV") and learners guess the verb before
formal presentation.

**Presentation (10 min).** Model: "Yesterday I worked in the morning.
I watched TV in the evening. I didn't study." Highlight `-ed` ending
rule and the negative pattern `didn't + base verb` (crucially: the
base verb, not the past form, after `didn't`).

**Guided practice (10 min).** Pair work: learners are given a picture
sequence of someone's yesterday and build 5 sentences together using
regular past verbs from the target list.

**Independent practice (10 min).** Learners write 5 true sentences
about their own real yesterday (at least 1 negative using `didn't`),
then share with a partner who asks one follow-up `Did you...?`
question (light preview of Lesson 7.2's question form).

**Speaking activity.** The partner sharing/follow-up-question exchange
above.

**Listening activity (5 min).** Listen to someone describing their
yesterday (6 sentences) and tick which of 8 activities they mention.

**Reading activity (5 min).** Read a short diary-style paragraph about
yesterday and answer 3 comprehension questions.

**Writing task (5 min).** Write a short diary entry (4-5 sentences)
about your real yesterday using regular past verbs.

**Pronunciation practice (5 min).** Drill the three pronunciations of
regular past `-ed` (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) using the target verb list —
directly building on Module 3's introduction of this same pattern for
present-simple `-s`.

**Vocabulary reinforcement.** Past-tense verb bingo (base form called,
learners mark the past form).

**Formative assessment.** Instructor checks correct `-ed` formation
and correct `didn't + base verb` during independent writing.

**Homework.** Learners write 3 more sentences about last night (not
yesterday daytime) using regular past verbs, ready for Lesson 7.2.

**Revision.** Lesson 7.2 opens with a rapid regular-past-verb recap
game before introducing irregular verbs.

**Extension activity.** Stronger learners identify and correct 2
deliberately-wrong `-ed` forms in a short error-correction exercise.

## Lesson 7.2 — "Last Weekend — Irregular Past Verbs & Questions"

**Learning objectives.** By the end of this lesson you can: (1) use 8+
common irregular past verbs correctly; (2) form simple past `yes/no`
questions (`Did you...?`) and short answers; (3) form simple past
`wh-` questions (`What did you...?/Where did you go?`); (4) narrate a
short weekend event in logical sequence.

**Prerequisite knowledge.** Lesson 7.1 (regular past, `didn't`).

**Warm-up (5 min).** Instructor tells one true irregular-verb-rich
sentence about their own last weekend ("I went to the market. I saw
my friend.") and learners identify which verbs are NOT `-ed` forms.

**Presentation (10 min).** Model: "Did you go out last weekend? Yes, I
did. I went to the beach. I saw my cousins. We had a great time!"
Present 8-10 common irregular verbs as a memorisable list (go→went,
see→saw, have→had, eat→ate, do→did, make→made, buy→bought, get→got).
Highlight the question pattern: `Did + subject + base verb` (never
`did + past-tense verb`).

**Guided practice (10 min).** Pair work: Learner A asks 4 `Did you...?`
questions using irregular verbs from a prompt list, Learner B answers
truthfully with short answers, then swap.

**Independent practice (10 min).** Learners interview a partner about
their real last weekend using at least 2 `wh-` questions
(`What did you do?`/`Where did you go?`) and report back one fact
about their partner to the class.

**Speaking activity.** The interview-and-report exchange above — this
lesson's core speaking task.

**Listening activity (5 min).** Listen to someone narrating their
weekend (6-7 sentences, irregular verbs included) and complete a
simple sequencing worksheet (what happened first, second, third).

**Reading activity (5 min).** Read a short weekend-diary text and
answer 3 `wh-` comprehension questions.

**Writing task (5 min).** Write 5-6 sentences narrating a real or
invented last weekend, using at least 3 irregular past verbs in
logical order.

**Pronunciation practice (5 min).** Drill natural sentence stress and
rhythm in short-answer forms ("Yes, I DID" / "No, I DIDn't") and
correct vowel sounds in commonly mispronounced irregular past forms
(went, saw, bought).

**Vocabulary reinforcement.** Irregular-verb pairs memory/matching
game (base form to past form).

**Formative assessment.** Instructor checks correct question
formation (`Did + base verb`) during the interview activity — the
lesson's most common target error.

**Homework.** Learners prepare 4-5 sentences about a real past holiday
or trip, ready for Module 7's assignment.

**Revision.** This lesson opens with the Lesson 7.1 regular-verb recap
noted above. Module 7's Quiz and Assignment draw on both lessons.

**Extension activity.** Stronger learners add one negative irregular
past sentence (`I didn't see...`) and one follow-up question to their
weekend narration.

## Module 7 Quiz (auto-graded, 8 questions)

*Seeded verbatim into `quiz_questions` — see
`sql/seed-curriculum-level-1.sql`. Answer key marked with ✓.*

1. "I ___ TV yesterday evening." — (a) watch (b) watched ✓ (c)
   watching (d) watches
2. "She ___ study last night." — (a) don't (b) doesn't (c) didn't ✓
   (d) isn't
3. "___ you go to the party?" — (a) Do (b) Does (c) Did ✓ (d) Were
4. "Yes, I ___." — (a) do (b) did ✓ (c) does (d) was
5. "We ___ to the beach last weekend." — (a) go (b) goed (c) went ✓
   (d) going
6. "What ___ you eat for breakfast?" — (a) did ✓ (b) do (c) does (d)
   were
7. Which is the correct past form of "buy"? — (a) buyed (b) bought ✓
   (c) buys (d) buying
8. "Two days ___ I visited my grandmother." — (a) before (b) last (c)
   ago ✓ (d) past

## Module 7 Assignment — "My Last Holiday"

**Instructions given to the learner:** Write (or record) 6-8 sentences
narrating a real (or invented, if preferred) recent holiday or trip.
Use at least 3 regular past verbs and at least 3 irregular past verbs.
Include at least one past-time expression (yesterday/last week/two
days ago) and present events in a logical time order.

**Grading rubric (for the instructor):**
- **Grammatical accuracy** — correct regular `-ed` forms, correct
  irregular past forms, correct negative form if used.
- **Vocabulary range** — at least 3 regular and 3 irregular verbs used
  correctly.
- **Task completion** — at least 6 sentences, at least one time
  expression.
- **Coherence** — events presented in a logical narrative sequence.

A grade at or above the platform's pass threshold marks Module 7
completed for the learner.
