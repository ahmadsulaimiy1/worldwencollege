Admission

Admission to the College

Admission is by application to the Registry followed by assessment. Applicants are placed at the level their attainment warrants rather than at the level they declare. No charge is made for assessment.

Procedure

The five stages of admission.

Application

The applicant writes to the Registry naming the faculty and programme sought, with an account of prior study.

Two working days

Assessment

Applicants to the Faculty of the Qur’ān are assessed by recitation before a member of faculty. Applicants to other faculties sit a written placement paper. Neither the application nor the assessment carries a charge.

One sitting

Offer of place

The Registry issues an offer naming the programme, the level of entry, the class, the timetable, the supervising member of faculty and the mode of instruction.

Within one week

Matriculation

The applicant signs the academic undertaking and is entered on the register of the College. There is nothing to pay: the College charges no tuition, and its only charges are for engrossed instruments, later and by choice.

On acceptance

Commencement

Classes begin at the start of the following teaching week. Admission is continuous through the session and does not wait upon the opening of a semester.

Applicants without formal certification

An applicant who has studied at a madrasah or under a teacher without receiving certification should state in the application where, with whom and in which texts the study was conducted. The Registry will assess the account and arrange examination at the stage the attainment warrants. Absence of a certificate is not a bar to admission at any level.

Applicants under eighteen

The graded certificates admit students from the age of ten, and the College publishes the conditions under which it teaches a minor before a parent enrols one. No member of faculty is ever alone with a student under eighteen in a private session; sessions are recorded and open to the guardian; anyone teaching a minor is vetted before the first session; and concerns go to a Safeguarding Officer who stands outside the teaching line. Individual instruction and the ijāzah route are closed to minors except where a guardian attends or the College appoints a second member of faculty, which it does without charge.

Applying

Application to the Registry.

Applications are received and assessed by the Registry directly. An acknowledgement is issued within two working days.

The application should state

  • Full name, country and city of residence
  • The faculty and programme sought
  • An account of prior study — where, with whom, and in which texts
  • Any certificates held, and the institutions that issued them
  • The hours a week available for study
  • Whether a supported place is sought

Settled in advance

What an applicant is told before paying, not after.

Four matters decide whether this College is usable by a particular person. Each is published here rather than left to be discovered at the first class.

The language you will be spoken to in

Explanation at the certificate levels is bilingual: Arabic is the object of study and increasingly the medium, with English carrying the explanation. Instruction at the Diploma and Higher Diploma is conducted in Arabic. That is stated at the outset so it is a goal to work toward and not an ambush in the third week. The support language of each class is named in the offer of a place.

Arrangements between men and women

Women are taught by women wherever a qualified teacher holds the specialism; no individual session between the sexes is scheduled as a matter of course, and where one is academically necessary the student chooses between a teacher of the same sex, a third person present, or a recorded session — at no cost. The camera is never required, in any class or any examination. The arrangements in full.

The placement assessment is not a test you can fail

It is free, untimed, may be resumed, may be taken again without limit, and is never reported as a score. It exists to answer the question that stops most people from applying — where would I even begin, and will I be embarrassed? — and an instrument that answered that with a mark would have failed at its only task. Nothing about admission carries a charge in any case.

There is no tuition fee, and nothing money can buy

The College charges nothing to teach, to examine, to re-examine, to hear an appeal, or to issue the digital certificate and transcript that are the award. Its charges are for engrossed instruments and an identity card, and none of them is a condition of anything. Individual instruction is allocated by the faculty on attainment and need, not on payment; and at the point of examination the examiner is not shown a candidate’s mode, charges or state of payment, because the record carries no such field (Reg. 9.2).
