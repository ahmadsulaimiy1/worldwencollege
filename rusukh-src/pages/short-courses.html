Ramaḍān

The College pauses its timetable and runs intensives instead.

Every other institution runs Ramaḍān as a normal teaching month with attendance quietly collapsing. We do the opposite: the general timetable stops, and the month is given to what it is actually for.

  • Ḥifẓ accelerates — daily sessions, double portions, for students already on a memorisation track.
  • Khatm circles — complete the Qur’ān in the month, recited in ḥalaqah, with correction.
  • Tafsīr of the juz’ — a nightly half-hour on the portion being read that night.
  • Taraweeh preparation — for those leading it, in the two weeks before.
Why this is in the academic calendar

Because a college of Qur’ānic sciences whose calendar does not reflect Ramaḍān is telling you something about itself. The academic year runs in three terms against the Hijrī calendar, and the Ramaḍān intensive sits between Term II and Term III as a published part of the year rather than an interruption to it.
