Academic Calendar

Three terms, kept against the Hijrī year.

With Gregorian dates published alongside, because our students live by both. Placement, however, runs continuously — you are never asked to wait four months for a term to turn before you can begin.

Why Ramaḍān is in the calendar rather than around it

Every other institution runs Ramaḍān as an ordinary teaching month and watches attendance quietly collapse. A college of Qur’ānic sciences whose calendar does not reflect Ramaḍān is telling you something about itself. We stop the general timetable and give the month to what it is for — and we publish it as a part of the academic year rather than an interruption to it.

Class times

All teaching times are published in West Africa Time (UTC+1), which does not shift through the year, with the UTC offset stated so that every student can convert once and be right permanently. The predecessor institution advertised “Nigeria 8:30 p.m. / UK 7:30 p.m.” — a pair of local clocks that is correct only during the months the United Kingdom is on GMT, and wrong for about seven months of every year.

Exact Gregorian dates for each term are issued to enrolled students and published here one term in advance. They are not shown for terms whose Hijrī dates have not yet been confirmed by sighting — a calendar that guesses is worse than one that waits.
