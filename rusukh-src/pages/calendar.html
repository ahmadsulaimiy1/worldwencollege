Academic Calendar

The session comprises three semesters kept against the Hijrī calendar, with Gregorian dates published alongside. Admission is continuous through the session and does not wait upon the opening of a semester.

Ramaḍān

The general timetable of the College is suspended for the month. The Ramaḍān programme — accelerated memorisation, khatm circles, nightly exegesis and preparation for Tarāwīḥ — is offered in its place, and forms part of the published session.

Class times

All class times are published in West Africa Time (UTC+1), which does not vary through the year, with the offset from Coordinated Universal Time stated so that students in other jurisdictions may convert once.

In the Riwāq, every time shown to a student is rendered in that student’s own zone, with no setting to get wrong. The student’s local time, the teacher’s and the Registry’s are three different things, and a college that displays only its own is asking every student abroad to do arithmetic before every class.

Sections, and the hour of Maghrib

A programme is one programme. Where demand supports it, it is scheduled in more than one section — West Africa, Europe, the Gulf, the Americas — with the same syllabus, the same gates and the same criteria. Not a second programme, not a second standard.

No live class is scheduled across the Maghrib window of its own section’s local time. A timetable that obliges a student to choose between the class and the prayer has been set by someone who was not thinking about the student.

Gregorian dates for each semester are issued to enrolled students and published one semester in advance. Dates are not published for semesters whose Hijrī commencement has not been confirmed.
