How to verify

Send us the reference number.

Every document this College issues carries a reference number on its face, in the form DAR-«year»-«award»-«serial». Send that number to the Registry and you will receive, by return:

  • Whether the document is genuine and currently valid
  • The holder’s name as recorded
  • The award, the date conferred, and the examining body
  • For an ijāzah: the full sanad as written on the document
  • Whether it has been revoked, and if so, when

We answer verification requests from anyone, without contacting the holder and without charge.

A document we will not issue

This College does not issue degrees, and any document presented as a Dār al-Rusūkh BA, MA or doctorate is a forgery — report it to the Registry. We confer the four awards of the certification ladder, course certificates, and ijāzah bi-sanad muttaṣil. Nothing else.
