Standards

The decisions register.

5 decisions have been taken. 25 are waiting. Presenting those as one list would claim settled positions the College does not have, so they are two lists and the waiting one is longer.

Awaiting

25 decisions nobody has taken.

Each is drafted with a recommendation and with the consequence of not deciding it. They fall into four groups, and the grouping explains why they are stuck.

Access

Who may hold what powers

Which roles may read a learner’s record, take financial actions, or erase data. Stuck because it requires an accountable officer, and none is appointed.

Assessment

Pass marks, resits, progression

The honours thresholds, the examination pass mark, the resit policy. Stuck because adopting an assessment standard without an external examiner would settle it in the wrong order.

Credentials

What may be conferred, and by whom

What a certificate may state, who may confer or withdraw an award, whether the graduate register is opt-in. Stuck behind the same appointment.

Data

Retention, erasure, and the permanent record

How long a learner’s voice recordings are kept, and what erasure means against a register that is meant to be permanent. Stuck because no Data Protection owner is appointed.

The pattern worth naming

Almost none of these is stuck for want of thought. Each is drafted, argued and ready. They are stuck for want of somebody with the authority to say yes — which makes the appointments the College is seeking the actual bottleneck, not the policy work.

Why This Is Public

A register nobody outside can read is an internal memo.

For a reviewer

It shows what has and has not been settled

A panel’s first question is which of an institution’s stated positions are actually decisions. This answers it without them having to ask.

For a student

It shows which rules could still change

Anything on this site marked as proposed appears here as awaiting. A learner is entitled to know which of the rules governing them is settled and which is a draft.

See the bodies that would decide these.
