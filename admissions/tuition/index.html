Tuition & Fees

Transparent pricing for the full IEFC pathway.

One programme fee, spread evenly across six levels — pay in full, by level, or (where approved) monthly.

Tuition Includes
  • All instructional units for the level
  • Live classes and recorded lessons
  • Learning resources and digital library access
  • Assessments and examinations
  • Progress reports and academic advising
  • Digital transcript
  • Digital certificate on successful programme completion
Additional Fees (Where Applicable)
  • Application fee
  • Admission processing fee
  • Registration fee
  • Graduation & certificate fee
  • Printed certificate (optional)
  • International courier (optional)
  • Certificate verification (optional)

Exact figures for optional and processing fees are confirmed at the point of application — ask Admissions for the current schedule.

Ways to Pay

Choose the plan that fits.

Full Programme

Pay the full $19,000 up front and complete enrolment for all six levels at once.

Per Level

Pay $3,166.67 at the start of each 4-month level as you progress.

Monthly Instalments

Spread each level's tuition across its four months, subject to institutional policy.
