The Application Journey

Five steps to enrolment.

  1. 01Enquire & choose your entry point — tell us your current English level and goals.
  2. 02Submit your application — the form below, plus identification and any prior English certificates you hold.
  3. 03Placement assessment — a short assessment confirms your correct starting level, Foundation through Advanced.
  4. 04Offer & enrolment — receive your offer, confirm your payment plan, and secure your place.
  5. 05Orientation & first live class — onboarding to the digital campus, then your first live session.
Start Your Application

Applications are currently processed directly by our Admissions team.

Our full online application will open inside the Student Portal as WEC-LC's digital campus goes live. Until then, send the details below to Admissions and we will personally guide you through placement and enrolment.

What to include in your enquiry

Full name · country of residence · current English level (if known) · which level or programme interests you · preferred start window.
