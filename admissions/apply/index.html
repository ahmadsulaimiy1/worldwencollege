Admissions

How to apply.

Five stages, described as they actually run. Where a stage is handled by a person rather than by software, this page says so — because that is what determines how long it takes and who you are dealing with.

Start Your Application Entry Requirements
The Journey

Five stages, and what each one really is.

The application record moves through a fixed set of states, and the state it is in is the honest answer to “where is my application?”. Those states are listed here in the order they occur.

StageWhat happensWho does itApplication state
1 · Estimate your level A one-question self-assessment on the Admissions page suggests a starting level. It is not the placement decision and it binds nobody, including you. You, in about thirty seconds—
2 · Submit the form Name, email address and country of residence, plus the self-assessed level if you took the estimate. Nothing else is asked for at this point — no documents, no fee. You. The record is created immediately and you are emailed a confirmation.submitted
3 · Placement A conversation and a short assessment to confirm which of the six levels you should enter. There is no automated placement test on this site; a member of the founding team arranges this with you by email. A person, not the platformplacement_pending
4 · Offer A written offer naming your confirmed entry level, the fee for it, and the payment options open to you. Admissionsoffer_sent → accepted
5 · Enrolment Payment is confirmed, an account is created, and your enrolment for that level begins. Your first lesson is available the same day. You and the platform togetherenrolled

An application can also end at withdrawn or rejected. Both are states the College records rather than deletes, so that a decision can be looked up later by whoever made it.

Timing

How long each stage takes, honestly stated.

WEC-LC has processed no applications at volume, so these are commitments rather than measured averages, and they are described that way.

Immediate

Submission

The record is created and the confirmation email is sent while you are still on the page. If the online form cannot be reached, it hands your details to your email application instead, so nothing is lost.

Committed

Placement contact within three working days

A commitment the College is making, not an average it has measured. If it is missed, write to Admissions and say so — a stated commitment that goes unenforced is worse than none.

Your pace

Offer to enrolment

An offer does not expire on a timetable, because there is no intake to fill. Take the time you need; nothing is lost by waiting and nothing is gained by hurrying.

Checking Your Application

You can look it up yourself.

Your reference

Keep the application id

The confirmation email carries an identifier beginning app_. It is the only key to your record, and it is deliberately the only key — the College will not disclose an application state to anyone who does not hold it, including someone who knows your email address.

No account needed

Status without signing in

You do not have an account at application stage — one is created at enrolment. So status is looked up by reference, not by login, and returns the state and the date it was created. Nothing else.

Before you pay, four things you are entitled to know

WEC-LC holds no accreditation. No External Examiner has been appointed, so no award has been conferred on anyone. No cohort has yet been taught. And no refund policy has been adopted — see Refunds, which says so plainly rather than leaving you to discover it. The full institutional position is at About · Institutional Status.
