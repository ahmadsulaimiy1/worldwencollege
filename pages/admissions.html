I Who WEC-LC Is For

Who the programme is built for, stated as five kinds of person rather than as one ideal one.

Five people, and what each of them is actually short of.

Naming an audience is not the same as understanding one. So rather than a list of labels, here is what each of these people is actually short of — because in every case it is the same thing, and in no case is it vocabulary.

i

The pupil whose school teaches English as a subject

They can pass the paper and cannot hold the conversation, because the paper never asked them to. What is missing is not knowledge; it is the hundreds of hours of production nobody has ever required of them. This programme requires it from Level I, and marks it by a person.

ii

The university student whose degree is taught in English

They follow the lecture and lose the seminar — the moment where marks are actually won, and where a point has to be made against someone who disagrees. Reason and Bearing are two of the six competencies here for exactly that reason.

iii

The professional who is competent and sounds junior

The most expensive gap on this list, and the least discussed. Their technical judgement is not in question; the register they can reach in English is, and it is the register that gets quoted in the room they were not in. That is a learnable thing, and Command and Judgement are what learning it is called here.

iv

The applicant with a test date and a real deadline

IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge preparation is embedded from Upper Intermediate onward — embedded, not bolted on, because a course that teaches the test and not the language produces a score that the first seminar contradicts.

v

The fluent speaker who wants the last ten per cent

Idiom, nuance, and the difference between being understood and being persuasive. Level VI exists for people who are already good and know precisely what they are still missing, which is usually the hardest thing on this page to teach and the easiest to describe.

Also here, and welcome on the same terms: secondary pupils, government employees, business executives, international students and study-abroad candidates. The five above are not an entry list — they are the five shapes the same gap takes.

X Progression & Recognition

Two ways into every level. The College’s own award, or somebody else’s.

You do not have to start at the beginning.

Each level is entered on the award below it, and the College will not pretend that is the only honest route. A learner who already reads at B2 should not be made to buy Levels I to III to prove it. So there are two doors into every level, and the second one recognises work you did somewhere else.

The internal route

The award for the level below, conferred by this College. Level I award opens Level II, and so on to Level VI. Nothing else is required and nothing else is charged for.

or

The recognition route

A recognised external qualification at the level below, or the College’s own placement assessment. Either satisfies entry outright. The placement assessment is free and is taken before you pay anything.

Every band below admits a learner directly to the level in its row. Two further routes, and the one limit on all of them

Prior certified study

A completed course elsewhere

A transcript from another institution showing taught, assessed study at the level below is read on its own terms: what was taught, what was assessed, and against what standard. The answer comes in writing with a reason, and a refusal names what was missing.

Recognition of Prior Learning

English you were never certificated for

Years of work conducted in English, schooling in an English-medium system, or life in an English-speaking country. RPL admits you to a level on demonstrated ability rather than paperwork, evidenced through the placement assessment and a spoken interview. It grants entry only.

The limit

Entry can be recognised. An award cannot

Every route here decides where you START. None of them shortens a level, waives an assessment or confers a WEC-LC award, and no credit is granted for a level you have not been taught and assessed in. An institution that awards its own certificate for somebody else’s work is selling paper.

Status of this policy

Adopted by the Executive and in force, on the same footing as the honours scheme. It is published in full before anyone is admitted under it, so that a candidate refused entry can read the standard they were measured against and argue with it.

XI Placement

How the entry level is decided, and why it is not decided by what you say about yourself.

How the entry level is decided.

Two things happen, in this order, and only the second one counts.

  1. 01Your own estimate. One question, six statements, thirty seconds. It exists so you can apply with a sense of where you are, and it is recorded on your application as a self-assessment — explicitly non-binding.
  2. 02The placement assessment. A short assessment and a conversation with a member of the founding team, arranged by email after you apply. It produces the level you actually enter.
If the placement turns out to be wrong

Say so in the first two weeks. A placement is a judgement made on limited evidence, and the evidence improves the moment you start studying. Moving a learner early costs the College nothing and saves them a term of the wrong material.

XII Practical Requirements

The machinery you need — four items, each earning its place.

What you need to actually study.

Each of these is listed because a specific part of the programme uses it, not because a requirements list looks thorough.

Device

A computer, tablet or phone

The learning platform runs in a browser. There is nothing to install and no minimum specification worth publishing — if it can play video, it can run the programme.

Connection

Enough to stream audio

Listening work is the spine of every level, so audio has to arrive. Video calls for live sessions want more; audio-only participation is accepted when a connection will not carry video, and that is a stated accommodation rather than a workaround.

Microphone

Any microphone

The Listening Lab asks you to record yourself and keeps the recordings so you and your teacher can hear the change over months. A phone or a laptop microphone is sufficient. This is the one requirement people are surprised by, which is why it is here rather than in a footnote.

Email

An address you read

Placement, offer and enrolment all move by email. It is the only channel the College currently relies on.

XVII Time Zones

A programme with no campus, handling being everywhere at once.

How a programme with no campus handles being everywhere at once.

Independent of the clock

The taught material

Lessons, exercises, listening work and assignments are studied when you can study them. This is the majority of the programme by time, and it has no schedule attached to it at all.

Dependent on the clock

Live sessions

Conversation practice and tutorials are live, which means somebody’s evening is somebody else’s morning. Sessions are timetabled against where enrolled students actually are, never against London’s clock, and recordings are the standing fallback — not a lesser option.

XVIII Language of Instruction

English throughout, with one exception the College names.

English throughout, with one exception.

Teaching

Conducted in English from Level I

Including at A1, where it is done with heavy support — visual, repetition, restricted language, and a teacher who slows down rather than translates. Teaching a language through the language is a method, not an obstacle, and Level I was written on that assumption.

The website

Available in Arabic

The public site is published in English and Arabic. The teaching itself is not translated: a translated lesson would be a different lesson, and the pronunciation and listening work in particular cannot survive translation.

XIX The Position

The visa position, stated without qualification of any kind.

Stated without qualification.

What WEC-LC does not do

The College is not a licensed student sponsor. It issues no Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies, no visa letter, no immigration attestation and no document that any immigration authority accepts as the basis of a study visa. It does not offer, and will not offer, assistance with immigration applications. Nothing on any other page of this site should be read as qualifying that.

Why

Because it teaches online

Study visas exist to permit physical presence for study. The IEFC involves no physical presence anywhere — there is no campus to attend. A visa is not withheld; it is simply not part of what this programme is.

Consequence

You study from home

Wherever you live, you study there. No travel, no accommodation, no relocation cost, and no immigration risk arising from the programme itself.

Exception

There is none

Not for any level, any fee arrangement, any nationality, or any sponsor. There is no case in which the College can help, and describing edge cases would only create hope where there is none.

XXI If Your Goal Is To Study Abroad

If your goal is to study abroad, what the programme can genuinely do for that.

What the programme can genuinely do for that.

Many applicants come to English study on the way to something else. That is a legitimate reason to be here, and it is worth being precise about what helps.

Can

Build the English a destination requires

The upper levels work directly on academic reading, academic writing, seminar discussion and the discourse of formal argument — the things that make the difference once you have arrived, and which a test score does not guarantee.

Can

Prepare you for the tests that are accepted

IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge preparation are built into the curriculum from Upper Intermediate onwards. Those tests, not this College’s certificate, are what admitting institutions and immigration systems currently recognise.

Cannot

Substitute for a recognised qualification

No External Examiner has been appointed and the College holds no accreditation, so the IEFC award should not be relied upon by anyone as an admission or immigration credential. That is the plain position and it will change only when it genuinely changes.
