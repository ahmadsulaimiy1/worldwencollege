What “London Campus” means

The College teaches the IEFC entirely online, worldwide. “London Campus” names where it is governed and administered — the office the Executive and the Registrar work from — and not a building anybody is taught in. Correspondence is by email, and every enquiry is answered by a named member of the College rather than by an automated queue.

Consultation

Speak to an academic adviser before you decide.

A consultation is a conversation with a member of the academic staff about your English, your purpose for it, and which level of the programme you would actually begin at. It costs nothing, commits you to nothing, and is offered in English and in Arabic.

One

Where you would start

The placement assessment names your CEFR level. The consultation explains what that level means for the thing you actually want to do with English — a degree, a licence, a board, a posting abroad.

Two

What it will take

Hours a week, months to the level you need, and the cost of getting there in your own currency. Told plainly, before you commit, so the plan is one you can actually keep.

Three

In your own language

Consultations are held in English or Arabic. More than 7,000 of the College’s learners are in the Gulf, and being advised in the language you think in is not a courtesy — it is the difference between advice and a brochure.
