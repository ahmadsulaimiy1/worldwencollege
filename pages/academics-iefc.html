Academic Progression

Six levels, mapped to the CEFR.

Each level is designed to build toward its corresponding Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) band — the benchmark most widely recognised by universities, employers and English-language institutions worldwide.

Assessment & Certification

Progress that's measured, not assumed.

Weekly quizzes and monthly assessments track progress within each level; a mid-level and a final examination confirm readiness to advance. Every level closes with a capstone project applying that level's skills to a real task.

  • Digital transcript updated after every level
  • Digital certificate on successful completion of the full programme
  • IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge English preparation embedded from the Upper Intermediate level onward
  • Continuous progress monitoring visible to students at every stage
