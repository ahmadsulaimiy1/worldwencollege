Academic workload and curriculum status

How the programme is measured. Each level carries 20 WEC Credits and a Total Qualification Time of 200 hours — 80 Guided Learning Hours and 120 Independent Learning Hours — across four months, or about twelve hours a week. The full programme is 120 WEC Credits and 1,200 hours. A WEC Credit is the College's own internal measure (one credit represents ten notional learning hours); it is not ECTS or CATS and carries no transfer entitlement to any institution.

These hours are a design figure, not a measurement. We will replace them with hours measured from real time-on-task once enough learners have completed a level, and we will say publicly where the measurement differs from the design. Total Qualification Time counts guided study plus the independent study expected within the programme; it does not count a learner's own wider exposure to English, which is substantial and is where much acquisition happens. It is not a claim that CEFR C2 can be reached in 1,200 hours from no English.

The Lessons column is the designed size of each level, not the amount of content published in the learning platform today. All sixty modules across all six levels are authored and live, with every module examination and assignment; the full complement of lessons within them is still being written and is released continuously. We publish the design figure because it is what the programme is being built to — and we tell you it is the design figure, because presenting it as delivered content would not be true.

Assessment & Certification

Progress that's measured, not assumed.

Weekly quizzes and monthly assessments track progress within each level; a mid-level and a final examination confirm readiness to advance. Every level closes with a capstone project applying that level's skills to a real task.
