I The Posts

Three, ordered by what each one unblocks rather than by seniority.

Three posts, in order of what they unblock.

Two of these can be fractional or consultancy engagements rather than salaried roles. That is stated because it is true, not to diminish them — a defined number of days from the right person is worth more here than a full-time appointment from the wrong one.

i

Academic Reviewer

MA TESOL, Applied Linguistics or equivalent, with assessment experience. A defined number of days reading a defined list. Every publication the College has produced is currently unreviewed by anyone who did not write it, and this post changes that more than any other.

Unblocks review
ii

Practising teacher

Any level, any hours. One teacher, one cohort, one term generates the classroom evidence the College entirely lacks — and which cannot be reasoned out, only observed.

Unblocks evidence
iii

External Examiner

Standard sector practice, engaged per cycle. The one post whose entire function is being outside the College, and the one that must be filled before any award can be conferred.

Unblocks conferral
II What Exists

Both columns, before the first conversation rather than after it.

What exists, plainly.

A candidate is spending their own standing on this. The second column below is the one that matters to them, so it is published with the same care as the first.

Exists today

6
  • Three cohorts taught since 2023, with awards conferred at Level I and Level II
  • Six levels and sixty modules, complete as a structure
  • Every lesson planned stage by stage, with timings and prerequisites
  • Every assessment written, with its criteria published before it
  • Published volumes covering curriculum, assessment and teaching
  • A platform built for this programme, with a record a learner owns

Does not exist

4
  • Accreditation
  • An External Examiner
  • Appointed board members — BASCE stands at 0
  • A graduate of the whole six-level programme

Every item in this column needs either somebody the College cannot appoint to itself or time it has not yet had. A candidate should know it before the first conversation, not after it.

III How to Apply

One address, no portal, no closing date.

Write, naming the post.

There is no application portal and no closing date. These posts are filled when the right person is found rather than on a schedule, and a first message is read by a member of the founding team rather than by a system.
