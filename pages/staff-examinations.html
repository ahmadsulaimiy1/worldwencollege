    The script

    Two readers, one published standard

    Mark the criterion, not the impression

    Each criterion is scored on its own descriptor, printed above the field it is scored in. There is no overall mark to type: the overall is the weighted arithmetic of what you enter, so it can never be a figure the criteria were fitted to afterwards.

    Three points, and two absolute cases

    Where the two readings fall within three percentage points, the first stands. Beyond that the markers reconcile in writing. A disagreement crossing an honours threshold or the fifty per cent floor is reconciled whatever its size — a point either side of a floor is no small thing to the candidate. The marking standard.

    Nothing is released from one pair of hands

    A mark reaches a candidate only when every criterion carries two readings, every reconciliation is settled in writing, and the spoken paper has been marked by a person. Both original readings stay on the record whatever the outcome. The examination regulations.
