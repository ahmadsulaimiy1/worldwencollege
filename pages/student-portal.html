Student Portal

One place for your classes, your work and your record.

The Student Portal holds every IEFC learner’s classes, assignments, progress and record in one place. Your credentials come from Admissions with your enrolment, and what follows is what is behind them.

What's Inside

Built around the student, level by level.

01

Dashboard

A live view of your current level, upcoming classes and outstanding assignments.

02

Live Classes

Conversation classes and tutorials, designed. The programme is taught asynchronously today, and sessions are recorded for anyone who cannot attend.

03

Assignments & Exams

Submit assignments and sit online examinations, with automatic grading where appropriate.

04

Your Progress

See your progress update as you move through each module. Attendance is not tracked — the programme is asynchronous, so it would measure the wrong thing.

05

Digital Library

Reading, listening and reference resources for your current level.

06

Transcripts & Certificates

A digital transcript after every level, and the level award with it. No External Examiner has been appointed, so every award is internally moderated — see Awards and honours.

07

Notifications

Stay on top of deadlines, results and announcements without checking in constantly.

08

Mobile-Friendly Access

The same portal, designed to work cleanly on a phone or tablet.

Your way in

The Portal opens with your enrolment.

Enrolled students receive their credentials from Admissions during onboarding. Everything above is behind that one sign-in — your programme, your week, your marks, your record and your statement of account.
