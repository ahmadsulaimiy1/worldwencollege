            Milestones

              This page reports what the College’s own measurement engine says about you, under the regulations named above, and about nobody else. Where a figure cannot be computed it says which record is missing rather than printing a zero in its place.

              I The instrument

              Four rules that govern every figure above, each published before any of it was measured.

              Nothing here is decided on the page.

              A module needs both halves

              Thirty per cent machine-marked and seventy per cent marked by a person, rounded once. There is no partial module mark, and a module never completes on one component — passing the quiz and never submitting the assignment leaves the module open.

              A condition is never met by silence

              Each one is true, false, or awaiting a record the College owes — and the third is not the second. Where a condition waits on the College’s own record, the page says so and marks the condition as the College’s, not yours.

              A classification never overrides a gate

              Every condition of an award is necessary. A level mark of 92 with a failed examination is not a Distinction and is not an award; it is a failed examination with strong coursework, and the record says exactly that.

              A milestone is earned, never given

              Each one names the evidence it was awarded on, and one not yet held names the shortfall rather than sitting blank. A milestone withdrawn keeps its reason on the record, because a withdrawal nobody can read the reason for is a deletion wearing a status column.

              If you think a mark is wrong

              Ask for a mark check within ten working days of release and the Registrar confirms that the published rubric was applied, that every criterion carries a score, and that the second marker read it — free, and answered in five working days. The full route, including appeal, is at Examinations and assessment, and the instrument every figure above is computed under is published at Academic regulations.
