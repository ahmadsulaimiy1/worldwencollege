I Inside

A few real pages, taken from the volume itself.

Not a mock-up, and not a flattering selection. 8 pages of Production Specifications — Trim, margin, type, paper, binding, taken at fixed fractions of its 19-page extent. Each one is rendered from the same file you would download.

II Contents

What is in this volume

  • Trim sizes and margins for each format the Press uses
  • Type sizes, leading and the measure each is set on
  • Paper stock and weight, and why each was chosen
  • Binding, and the spine calculation that follows from the extent
III Use

What it lets you do

Reprint any volume to the specification it was set to

Send a WEC Press file to a printer without a conversation

Reproduce the house format for a document of your own
