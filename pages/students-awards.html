III What the Award Is Worth

Exactly what it says, and nothing you have to guess at.

A credential is worth what you can find out about it. Most institutions leave you to infer that; this one writes it down. Here is the whole of it — who conferred these awards, on what evidence, and the one confirmation nobody has yet given them.

What was conferred

Level I and Level II, on named students

{{S:CONFERRED}} Each was conferred against a dated enrolment record, a full set of assessment results and a transcript the College still holds and will produce for its holder. Nothing was conferred on attendance, on payment, or on time served.

What is missing

No External Examiner is appointed

An external examiner exists to be outside the College — to confirm its standards are what it says they are, by someone with nothing to gain from the answer. These awards were set, marked and second-marked inside the College. That makes them internally moderated, and the College writes the word rather than leaving you to assume the stronger one.

What will not be published

No rates, no outcomes, no borrowed figures

{{S:FIGURES}} Employment outcomes are not published either, and will not be until the College can survey people whose careers have had time to move. A statistic quoted early is a statistic quoted forever.
