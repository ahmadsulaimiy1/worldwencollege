Awards and honours.

Six awards, one for each level, each with its own title and post-nominal. Two of them are held by named students of this College. This page says who moderated them, and who did not.

I The Awards

One for each level.

Each award names a real standing rather than decorating a completion. The first is deliberately modest, because the first is where most people stop. Every one of them is conferred on the same terms whether the candidate was taught by the College or took the level independently — same examination, same rubric, same second marking, same registry entry.

II Honours

Adopted, and applied to nobody.

The scheme below was adopted by the Executive on 14 August 2026, subject to ratification by the Academic Senate once that body has appointed members. It is in force, and it has been applied to nobody: it postdates every award this College has conferred. The students holding a Level I or Level II award passed against the standard in force when they sat it, and no honour is being applied to them retrospectively. A grade awarded backwards is a grade nobody earned.

Pass

70% overall, with no skill below 60%.

Merit

80% overall, with no skill below 70%.

Distinction

88% overall, with no skill below 80%.

High Distinction

94% overall, with no skill below 88%.

Distinction of the College

Conferred by decision, never calculated — and may be conferred in no cycle at all. It is named for the institution because the College has no Chancellor.

These are the thresholds themselves, published so that a candidate can read the standard before sitting to it. No academic distinction under this scheme has been conferred on anyone.

III What the Award Is Worth

Exactly what it says, and nothing you have to guess at.

A credential is worth what you can find out about it. Most institutions leave you to infer that; this one writes it down. Here is the whole of it — who conferred these awards, on what evidence, and the one confirmation nobody has yet given them.

What was conferred

Level I and Level II, on named students

{{S:CONFERRED}} Each one was conferred against a dated enrolment record, a full set of assessment results across all four skills, and a transcript the College still holds and will produce for its holder on request. Nothing here was conferred on attendance, on payment, or on time served: the College never built a mechanism that could, and it does not intend to build one.

What is missing

No External Examiner is appointed

An external examiner exists to be outside the College — to confirm its standards are what it says they are, by someone with nothing to gain from the answer. These awards were set, marked and second-marked inside the College. That makes them internally moderated, and the College writes the word rather than leave you to assume the stronger one. It is the largest single thing this page cannot yet tell you, and it is written here rather than left out.

What will not be published

No rates, no outcomes, no borrowed figures

No completion rate, and no outcome statistic. A rate calculated across eight cohorts is a number pretending to be a trend, and an employment figure needs careers that have had time to move. Both are published when they mean something and not before, because a figure quoted early is quoted forever. Published instead: every rubric, every pass mark and every criterion the awards were judged against, so you can weigh the standard itself rather than take a percentage on trust.
