What a publishable rubric has to hold

The weights sum to one

A composite that does not sum to its whole is not the composite the rubric describes, and a mark computed from it would be a mark against a standard nobody published.

All four skills are measured

The published skill floor is read against all four examination sub-marks. A paper that measures three of them refuses conferral to every candidate who sits it, for a fault that is the paper’s and not theirs.

One criterion is spoken

Every level examination carries a spoken component of at least fifteen minutes, and the spoken gate needs a criterion to read. A programme that marks speaking from written work is not marking speaking.
