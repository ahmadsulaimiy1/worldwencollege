IEFC · Level VI

English Mastery Programme

Mastery is not more English. It is English used with a personal voice: style that is chosen rather than inherited, argument built for a specific audience, and the confidence to work in registers — academic, professional, public — that require judgement rather than rules. It is the level at which the learner stops being a student of the language.

Assessment

How the level is judged.

Every instrument is written before the teaching it tests, and the criteria are published to the learner in advance.

Continuous

10 module quizzes

One at the end of each module, testing the language that module taught and nothing else. Marked automatically, with the correct answer and the reason shown.

Productive

10 assignments

Speaking and writing tasks marked by an instructor against a published rubric. These are where the productive skills are actually assessed — a quiz cannot judge whether someone can hold a conversation.

Self-check

Before every assessment

Each lesson carries a self-check with traps aimed at the mistakes learners at this level really make, so a learner discovers a gap before an examiner does.

Teaching Methods

How it is taught.

Speaking-first

Practice before polish

Guided speaking is built into every lesson, not offered as an extra. Learners speak from the first lesson, before their grammar is ready, because waiting for accuracy is how people stay silent for years.

Structured

A shared standard, not a personal style

Every instructor teaches to the same mapped curriculum, so a learner who changes class or cohort does not lose their place, and two learners at the same level have covered the same ground.

Supported

A teacher who knows what goes wrong

Instructors work from the Teacher's Companion, which sets out for each lesson what commonly goes wrong, a second way to explain it, and what to do for the learner who is behind and the one who is ahead.

Measured

Adjusted to the cohort

Quiz and assignment results are visible to the instructor as the level runs, so teaching responds to how this group is actually performing rather than to how the plan assumed they would.

The Award

Worldwide English Proficiency Certificate

The College's highest qualification: comprehensive proficiency in English · Post-nominal WEPC

What it honours

Why this award exists

carries the College's own name because it is the qualification the College is willing to be judged by. It does not say Certificate in something; it says proficiency, without qualification, because at this stage there is no remaining domain of English in which the holder is a learner. It is the only qualification in the framework named for the institution, and that is deliberate: a holder of the WEPC is presenting Worldwide English College's judgement of them, not a module count.

Graduate profile

What the holder can do

The capstone level. The learner refines toward near-native command: spontaneous, precise, and persuasive across virtually any register or context, capable of leading, teaching, and representing an organisation in English at the highest level.

What this award is not

WEC holds no accreditation, and the College has not appointed an External Examiner — the independent post required before any award can properly be conferred. This award is defined, its criteria are published, and it has been conferred on nobody. See About · Institutional Status.

Questions

What people ask about this level.

Duration

How long does Level VI take?

4 months of study by design, covering 120 designed lessons across 10 modules. Learners who need longer are not penalised; the level is a body of work, not a race.

Entry

Do I need Level V first?

Not necessarily. You need the language Advanced Programme teaches, however you acquired it. A placement assessment establishes that.

Assessment

What happens if I fail an assessment?

Assessments can be resat. The purpose is to establish what you can do, not to record a single bad afternoon — the appeals and resit procedure is published in full.

Recognition

Is the award recognised?

The award is defined and its criteria published, but WEC holds no accreditation and has appointed no External Examiner. We state this plainly rather than implying recognition the College has not obtained.
