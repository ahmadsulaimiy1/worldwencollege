    I The roll

    Who appears, on whose consent, and what a reader may rely on.

    Four rules govern this page.

    Every award, at every level

    The International English Fluency Course confers an award at each of its six levels, and all of them are recorded here. A register that listed only its highest graduates would say by omission that the other awards were steps rather than achievements.

    Listed by consent

    A graduate is published here only after agreeing to be. Consent governs this page alone: it does not govern verification, because a verification code is something a graduate chose to hand someone, and honouring it is the entire purpose.

    Every entry is checkable

    Each listing carries its verification code and links to its own record. A register of names that could not be checked would be a claim on a webpage; the College’s position is that its public claims are verifiable, and that has to include this one.

    Verification is the authority

    An award that has been withdrawn or superseded leaves this roll and stays in the Register. Its verification page continues to resolve and states its standing, with the date. This page is the roll; verification is the answer.
