    What this desk does

    Mark what has been handed in

    Written work and spoken work, oldest first, each carrying the rubric it was set against and the mark it is a resit of. Go to marking.

    Read a learner you teach

    Your roster, one learner’s engagement beside the platform’s own reading of it, a register you may take yourself, and the confirmation that a level is finished. Go to your learners.

    Publish the hours you are free

    An hour a learner can book, withdrawn with a reason that reaches everyone holding a place in it. Go to your hours.

    Address a notice, and answer a letter

    A notice to your level in either language, drafted, published, amended or withdrawn with the reason on the record — and the threads your learners have open with you. Go to notices.

    Answer a case you are not part of

    The queue in the order each answer falls due, with the accounts that may not hear each case named beside it — so a case reaches the right person the first time. Go to cases.

    And what it will not do

    It will not show you a learner you do not teach, publish an hour under a colleague’s name, let you answer a case you decided, or record an act against anyone but the account signed in. Those are refusals in the platform, not conventions on this page. The tutor’s handbook.
