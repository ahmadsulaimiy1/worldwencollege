Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the IEFC programme, admissions, and studying with WEC-LC.

The IEFC is WEC-LC's English programme — its only one — structured as six CEFR-aligned levels (A1 through C2), each carrying 10 modules, 20 WEC Credits and a Total Qualification Time of 200 hours. The full programme is 120 WEC Credits and 1,200 hours. Total qualification time is a design figure, not a measurement — it is the workload the curriculum was built to, and how many months it takes depends on the hours you can give it each week.

Studying

What it is actually like.

Your material is available the day you enrol. There is no intake date and no term to wait for, and study is paced by you rather than by a timetable — see Dates.
Each level is designed at 200 total qualification hours, 1,200 across all six. How many months that is depends on the hours you can give it each week. The College publishes the hours rather than a number of months it cannot stand behind.
A device that plays video, a connection that carries audio, and a microphone — the last because the Listening Lab asks you to record yourself so that change over months can be heard. A phone microphone is sufficient.
A transcript is issued after each level, and a level award follows it — students hold the Level I and Level II awards. Every award is set, marked and second-marked inside the College: no External Examiner has been appointed, so the moderation is internal. See Quality assurance.
