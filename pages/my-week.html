      I The clock

      Four rules about time, each of them a decision the College made rather than a default it inherited.

      A time is only a time if you know whose clock it is on.

      Every instant carries four things

      The moment in UTC, the same moment in your zone, the offset between them, and the zone’s name. A class time rendered in one zone and read in another is a learner who misses the class, and this College teaches across sixty of them.

      An empty week is an answer

      Nothing scheduled, an enrolment waiting on payment, and no enrolment at all are three different facts. The feed says which one applies rather than showing you the same blank panel for all three.

      A file, and the College says it is a file

      Your calendar keeps a copy of the feed as it stands when you download it. A live subscription would need a calendar client to sign in, which none of them do, so the College gives you the file and tells you what it is rather than offering a link that quietly stops updating.

      A cancellation is attributed

      Giving back a place takes a reason, and the reason is recorded as yours. That is what keeps a tutor’s withdrawal from ever reading as your cancellation on your own record, which is the whole point of writing it down.

      Times are shown in the zone on your account

      Change it and every time on this page moves with it. If the College holds no zone for you the feed falls back to UTC and says so — it is the one zone that cannot be wrong about itself, and it is a fallback rather than a guess.
