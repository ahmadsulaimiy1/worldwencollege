                What this cannot see

                  This record is yours and is bound to your session. It describes what reached the College; it is not a mark, not a condition of any award, and never a penalty.

                  I The measure

                  Four decisions about measuring a learner, each of them a limit the College put on itself.

                  A correction that hides what it corrected is not a record.

                  The week is yours, not the calendar’s

                  A window is seven days anchored to the day your own enrolment started, so a learner who began on a Wednesday is measured Wednesday to Wednesday. Weeks that start on Monday for everybody would penalise whoever enrolled on a Friday.

                  Nothing is inferred from a tab left open

                  Every state names the evidence it was read from: minutes the server measured, an assessment attempted, laboratory practice submitted, a lesson completed, or a live session a host confirmed. An issued join link is not a person in a room.

                  A quiet week is a description

                  Engagement is not a mark, not a participation grade, not a measure of learning and not a condition of any award. A window with nothing in it says what reached the College, and the College says so rather than colouring it as a failure.

                  A person may write a state, and be seen doing it

                  A tutor can record something the platform could not see, and the platform’s own reading of that same window stays on the record beside it with the reason given. That is what makes the correction checkable rather than simply final.

                  The limits are published with the measurement

                  Study time is held as one running total per module rather than a ledger, nothing observes who joins a live session, and work done away from the platform is invisible to it. All three are printed on this page beside your own record, because a measurement whose limits are private is one you cannot argue with.
