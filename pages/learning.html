Learning

What a week actually looks like.

Most of the programme is studied when you can study it. A smaller part is live and depends on other people. This page separates the two, because that separation is what makes an online programme workable or not.

The Platform The Six Levels
The Shape Of A Week

Five kinds of work, in the proportion they occur.

The bulk

Working through lessons

Each lesson is staged, with the stages timed, and can be paused and resumed. This is where most of the hours go and none of it is scheduled.

Daily, briefly

Listening and recording

Short and frequent beats long and occasional for both listening and pronunciation. The Lab is built for ten minutes a day rather than an hour a week.

Weekly

An assignment

One produced thing per module — written, spoken or done — marked by a person against a rubric you saw before you started.

Before assessment

Self-checking

Not marked and not recorded against you. They exist so you can find out what you do not know at no cost.

Live

Conversation and tutorials

The part that depends on other people being present. Recorded for anyone who cannot attend, and recordings are not treated as the lesser option.

At the end

The level assessment

The summative point, against criteria published from the start of the level.

Self-Paced, With A Caveat The College Will State

Self-paced study has a known failure mode.

Said plainly, because it affects you

Wholly self-paced language study finishes badly, and the reason is well understood: no fixed points, no peers at the same stage, nothing to be late for. WEC-LC currently runs self-paced, because that is what is built. The College has drafted a recommendation to add a fixed rhythm of live sessions, examination windows and orientation on top of it, and that recommendation has not been adopted — see Dates. Until it is, the structure has to come from you, and you should plan for that rather than discover it in month four.

What helps

A fixed hour, not a target number of hours

A learner who studies at the same time daily finishes; a learner who intends to do six hours a week does not. This is the single most useful thing anyone can tell you about studying alone.

What helps

Little and often, for listening especially

Ten minutes daily beats seventy on Sunday for anything involving the ear. The programme is arranged to make that possible.

What the College does

Notices, and makes contact

Engagement is tracked so that someone who has gone quiet is reached in month two rather than discovered in month eleven. It never produces a penalty — see Support.
