Governance · WorldWide English College

Every rule this College operates under, and the authority behind each one.

A panel’s first question is never "what is your policy on X". It is "what are your instruments, and on whose authority does each one stand". This page answers both, for all twenty-four, without anybody having to ask.

Twenty-four instruments govern this College: the regulations that decide a mark, the handbooks that gather what one person is bound by, the codes, the procedures a learner invokes, and the policies under which the place is run. Each is published in full, in both editions, and each is named here with what it governs, the body that owns the subject matter and the body that adopted the text.

VII The Authorities

Three bodies, and which of them owns what.

Who may make a rule, and who may change one.

Ownership and adoption are different acts, and this register keeps them apart. The body that owns a subject cannot be assumed to have written the text, and the body that wrote the text does not thereby own the subject.

Article I

The Board of Governors

Owns the academic standard and the framework beneath it. No member of the Executive may alter either.

Article II

The Academic Senate

Academic judgement — marks, progression and awards. They may not be overturned on administrative or commercial grounds.

The College Executive

Daily operation, under policy set by the Board. It is the College's constituted decision-making authority, and every instrument in this register was adopted by it.

The Institutional Quality Commission

Academic quality, institutional effectiveness and the review cycle. It recommends; the Executive adopts.

VIII The Sittings

Three sittings, and what was adopted at each.

Where a date comes from, when there is one.

7 of the 24 instruments state a date of adoption. The dates below are the three sittings on the record at the Decisions Register, and every stated date is one of them. The other 17 are published under the standing authority of the Executive, which is the College’s constituted decision-making body, and this register prints that authority without a date rather than a date without a minute.

What this register does not do

It does not date an instrument the College has not dated, it does not promote an instrument awaiting Senate ratification to settled academic policy, and it does not name an external body as having reviewed any of this. Where a column is empty it is empty because the fact is not held, and that is the column doing its job.
