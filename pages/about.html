Our Operating Model

Online-first by design. London-based by administration.

"London Campus" identifies WEC-LC's administrative and management headquarters — it is not a claim of a physical teaching campus, and it never referred to more than one. Our educational delivery is intentionally online-first: every level of the IEFC is taught through live and recorded instruction inside our digital campus, so a student in Lagos, Riyadh, or Manila studies on equal footing with one in London. This is a deliberate strategic choice for global accessibility and flexibility, not a stand-in for premises we don't yet have.

What "London Campus" means

The institution's administrative and management headquarters, and the seat of its governance — the brand and legal home of WEC-LC.

What "London Campus" doesn't mean

A physical building where classes are taught, or a network of regional campuses. Every student, everywhere, studies through the same online digital campus.
