Digital Learning Environment

A premium Learning Management System, built around the student.

Every IEFC student learns inside a dedicated digital campus. See the full walkthrough on the Student Portal page.

Learning & Assessment

Student dashboard, interactive learning portal, assignment submission, online examinations, automatic grading where appropriate.

Admissions & Records

Online admissions, enrolment, progress tracking per learner, and digital transcripts. Attendance is deliberately not tracked: the programme is asynchronous, so attendance would measure the wrong thing — engagement is what triggers support.

Resources & Certification

Digital library, notifications, digital certificates, and mobile-friendly access from anywhere.
