About

How the College is organised.

This page describes posts and functions, not people. Where a post is filled a register names the holder — the faculty register for teaching staff, the governance register for the Board, the Senate and the Executive — and where it is vacant this page says vacant.

Three Functions

Academic, administrative, publishing.

Academic

Teaching and standards

Academic staff and tutors deliver the programme; the two academic bodies — BASCE and SENATE — hold standards and the competency framework. The roster is published on the Faculty page.

Administrative

Admissions, records, finance

Admissions, student records, assessment administration and finance. At the College’s current size these are designed to be held by few people rather than many, and the platform’s access levels reflect that.

Publishing

WEC Press

The College’s imprint publishes the curriculum, assessment and teaching volumes. It has no separate legal personality and no staff of its own, and every volume says so.

Separation of Duties

Three separations that are not negotiable.

These exist only to keep certain people independent of each other. They are the first thing an accreditation panel looks for, they cost nothing to preserve now, and they are expensive to unpick later.

First

Author &ne; Academic Reviewer

Nobody reviews their own work. A publication reviewed by its author has not been reviewed.

Second

Reviewer &ne; External Examiner

The examiner’s whole function is being outside the College. It cannot be doubled with an internal role.

Third

Conferring &ne; examining

Whoever confers an award is not whoever examined it.

What this means for staffing

The minimum credible academic staffing is two qualified people who are not each other, plus one external. Everything else at this size can legitimately be combined — records with assessment administration, finance with procurement, IT with learning technologies.

Vacant

External Examiner

Blocks conferral

Independent of the College by definition. No award can properly be conferred until it is filled.

Academic Reviewer

Blocks review

Every published volume is authored and unreviewed by anyone who did not write it.
