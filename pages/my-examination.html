If something goes wrong

A dropped connection is the College’s problem

Your work is written to the server as you produce it. A paper reopened within sixty minutes of a break resumes with the time that was left on it. A longer break sets the attempt aside at your election, it leaves no mark on the record, and a fresh window opens in three working days.

A bad week is a claim, not a failure

A claim of mitigating circumstances reaches the Registrar within twenty working days of the mark being released, and is decided by three people none of whom marked the work. Where no document exists — a power cut, an unsafe situation at home — a signed account of it is enough. Open one here.

A mark you dispute is checked, free

Ask within ten working days of release and the Registrar confirms that the published rubric was applied, that every criterion carries a score, and that the second marker read it. An appeal is a different thing — it is against the way a decision was reached. Both are set out here.
