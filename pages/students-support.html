When you are stuck.

Being stuck, falling behind and being unhappy with something are three different problems with three different answers. This page gives all three, and says plainly what the College does not provide.

Stuck On The Material

Built into the teaching before you have to ask.

In the lesson

A second explanation, already written

Every lesson carries an alternative explanation of its hardest point, written in advance for the learner who did not follow the first one. Teaching that has only one route through it fails everyone who needs a different one.

In the lesson

The mistakes people actually make

Each lesson names the common errors for that point and what causes them, so that a mistake is recognisable rather than mysterious. These are written from the language itself, not gathered from students — the College has taught no one yet, and says so.

On demand

A tutorial with a teacher

Where the written support does not resolve it, a tutorial does. Ask; it is not rationed.

Falling Behind

Noticed early, and answered with help.

Why it is measured

To reach you, never to penalise you

Engagement is tracked so that a learner who has gone quiet is contacted in month two rather than discovered in month eleven. It never produces a penalty, and there is no attendance requirement to fail — the programme is asynchronous, so attendance would be the wrong measure of anything.

What happens

A message, an offer, a conversation

Falling behind produces contact, not consequence. Someone who is behind can still finish; the entire value of noticing is reaching them while that is true.

Unhappy With Something

Who answers, and how honestly.

Who

The founding team, by name

Every message to the College is answered by a member of the founding team. There is no ticket queue and no first-line script, which is a genuine advantage of the College’s size and will not survive growth — so it is described as it is now.

What is missing

A formal complaints procedure

There is no independent stage to escalate to, because there is no appointed body to escalate to. That is stated on Academic regulations as well, because it is the kind of gap that should not be findable in only one place.

What The College Does Not Provide

Named, so that nobody relies on it.

No

Counselling or wellbeing services

WEC-LC has no counselling provision, no wellbeing service and no qualified staff for either. If you need that kind of support, it must come from services where you live. Implying otherwise on a website is how people in difficulty get let down.

No

Careers or immigration advice

No careers service, and no immigration advice of any kind — see Visas and study permits.

No

Disability assessment or formal adjustments

The College has no process for assessing a need or granting a formal adjustment. What it can do is arrange practical accommodations informally — audio-only participation, extended time, alternative formats — on request. That is a smaller offer than a policy, and it is described as the smaller thing it is.
