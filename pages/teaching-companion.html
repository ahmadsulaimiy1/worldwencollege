Teaching

The Teacher’s Companion.

A printed volume covering every lesson of Level I, in which each panel is marked with where its knowledge came from. It is the teaching support record in a form a teacher can hold while teaching.

The Volume

What it contains.

Every lesson

All 0 of Level I

Each with its stages and their declared minutes in the running head, so a teacher can see the shape of the lesson without turning back to the plan.

Every panel

Marked with its provenance

DERIVED, ESTABLISHED or DESIGNED, printed on the panel itself rather than explained once in a preface. A teacher disagreeing with a panel can see immediately whether they are disagreeing with the curriculum, with the field, or with the authors.

One mark absent

OBSERVED

The fourth mark does not appear anywhere in the book, and the front matter says why. A companion that quietly dropped the category would be claiming a kind of authority it does not have.

How It Is Made

Generated from the record, not written alongside it.

One source

The book cannot disagree with the curriculum

It is composed directly from the same record the platform teaches from. A companion maintained by hand drifts from the programme within one revision, and every teacher who trusts it then teaches something the College no longer says.

Counted, not asserted

The figures in the front matter are measured

The number of panels of each kind is counted from the pages as composed, not from the database that fed them — because some entries render in a running head rather than as a panel, and a front matter that said otherwise would be wrong in a way nobody would ever check.

Availability

How to obtain a copy.

Not a download on this site

The Companion is a typeset volume produced by WEC Press and is not published for download here. Teachers, reviewers and anyone assessing the College’s academic work can request a copy from info@worldwencollege.co.uk. It is offered particularly to anyone willing to review it — every volume the College has produced is currently unreviewed by anyone who did not write it.
