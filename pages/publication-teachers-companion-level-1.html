II Contents

What is in this volume

  • For every Level I lesson: what commonly goes wrong, and why
  • A second way to explain each point, for when the first does not land
  • What to do for the learner who is behind, and the one who finished early
  • A front matter that states plainly what the volume does not contain

What it lets you do

Prepare a Level I lesson in the time between two classes
