II What It Is For

An imprint exists to make claims checkable, not to make an institution look larger.

An imprint exists to make claims checkable.

A curriculum that lives only in a database can be changed without anyone noticing. A printed volume cannot.

One

To fix a version

A volume is a version of the programme that can be held, dated and disagreed with. Once printed, a claim cannot be quietly revised out of existence — which is precisely what makes it worth reviewing.

Two

To be examined by outsiders

An accreditation panel, an external examiner or a sceptical teacher can be handed a book. They cannot be handed a login and a promise. Publication is how a new institution makes itself inspectable.

Three

To carry its own provenance

Every volume states what it rests on and what it does not. That is a house rule, not a preference — see House standards.

III The Rule It Publishes Under

One rule, and it forbids the thing every young press does first.

Never publish to raise the count.

The standing instruction

No volume is produced to increase the number of volumes. If a proposed title would not improve the education — a learner’s study, a teacher’s lesson, an examiner’s marking — it is not built. Two planned titles have been formally withdrawn under this rule and their withdrawal is recorded rather than forgotten.

Why the rule is needed

A publishing programme rewards volume

Once a catalogue exists, the easiest way to make it look stronger is to add to it. The rule exists because that pressure is real and does not announce itself.

What it costs

A shorter list than the College could produce

The register behind the Press holds 74 candidate resources. Producing all of them is possible; most of them would not improve anything, and the catalogue is shorter for it.

IV The Position

Produced, and unreviewed. Said here rather than discovered later.

Produced, and unreviewed.

Done

11 volumes

Composed, typeset, proofed and produced. Each one is a real object that can be requested and read today.

Not done

Reviewed by anyone independent

No Academic Reviewer has been appointed. Every volume the College has produced was reviewed, if at all, by the people who wrote it — which is to say not reviewed. This has its own page rather than a footnote.

V Generated From The Record

Never maintained alongside it — the difference decides whether a publication can drift from the truth.

Generated from the record, never maintained alongside it.

What it prevents

A book that disagrees with the programme

A volume maintained by hand drifts from the curriculum within one revision, and every teacher who trusts it then teaches something the College no longer says. Composition from the source makes that drift impossible rather than unlikely.

What it costs

The book cannot be improved in the book

Improving a sentence means improving the record it came from, which is slower and occasionally infuriating. It is also the only arrangement in which the improvement reaches the learners as well as the readers.

VI One Typographic System

Four formats, one set of rules, so a reader can tell a College title by its type.

One typographic system, four formats.

A house is recognisable because it is consistent, and consistency at this scale is enforced by a shared system rather than by remembering.

Reference

For consulting

Companions and handbooks — opened at a point rather than read through, so the page is built to be scanned and returned to.

Practice

For working in

Workbooks, where the page has to leave room for a learner to write and still hold its structure.

Flagship

For the whole programme

The curriculum in full, where the reader’s problem is navigation across hundreds of pages rather than density on one.

Scholarly

For examination

Institutional and architectural volumes, set for a reader who is checking rather than learning.

VII Provenance

Every claim carries where it came from.

Every claim carries its provenance.

On the page

Not in the preface

Where a volume states something a teacher will act on, the source of that statement is marked on the panel itself. A provenance note explained once in the front matter is a provenance note nobody reads.

Including absence

A category that appears nowhere

The Teacher’s Companion marks each panel as derived, established or designed. The fourth mark — observed in a classroom — appears nowhere in the book, and the front matter says why rather than quietly dropping the category.

Counted, not asserted

The figures are measured from the pages

Front-matter counts are taken from the composed pages rather than from the database that fed them, because some entries render in a running head instead of as a panel. A count taken from the wrong place would be wrong in a way nobody would ever check.

VIII Peer Review

Authored and reviewed by the same people is not reviewed.

Authored and reviewed by the same people is not reviewed.

What was done

Internal checking, and a great deal of it

Volumes are proofed, cross-checked against the record, and verified automatically for internal consistency — sequence, arithmetic, terminology, coverage. That catches contradictions and errors of fact.

What it cannot catch

Being wrong in a way the authors share

A shared assumption is invisible from inside. Whether a lesson teaches what it claims to teach, whether an assessment measures what it says it measures, whether the level is pitched where it says it is — none of that can be established by the people who decided it.

IX Why It Is Not Simply Fixed

It requires a person, and the post is open.

It requires a person, and the post is open.

The post

Academic Reviewer

MA TESOL, Applied Linguistics or equivalent, with assessment experience. A defined number of days reading a defined list. It can be a consultancy engagement rather than a salaried role, and it is the appointment that would change more than any other.

The separation

Author is not reviewer, ever

The College treats this as non-negotiable. Appointing an author to review their own work would produce a page saying “reviewed” and change nothing about the volumes, which is worse than the present position because it would be false.

The invitation

The volumes are offered for review

Any suitably qualified reader willing to review a volume is offered a copy. Write to info@worldwencollege.co.uk. Criticism is the point of the exercise, not a risk of it.

X The Commitment

What will happen when review begins, written before it does.

What will happen when review begins.

Published

What review overturns, as an overturning

Findings will be recorded against the volumes they concern, visibly. A review whose findings all confirm the author is a review nobody is actually reading.

Not retrofitted

No volume will be marked reviewed retrospectively

Volumes produced before review remain volumes produced before review. Their status will be stated on their own record rather than quietly upgraded by a later appointment.

XII What Is Blocking It

Nothing that can be written. The blocker is a person, not a document.

Nothing that can be written.

Every remaining item requires a person, a studio or an outside body. That is why the resource figure and the stage figure separated.

Requires a studio

The recorded audio

The listening scripts are written, marked and published as a volume. The recordings do not exist. Producing them needs voices and a room, and it is the largest single item standing between the programme and first delivery.

Requires a classroom

Observation evidence

Nothing in the record has been observed in teaching. One teacher, one cohort, one term produces evidence that cannot be reasoned out — see Development and observation.

Requires an outside body

External examining and review

The award cannot be conferred and the volumes cannot be reviewed by anyone inside the College. Both are appointments, not tasks.

XIII Withdrawn

Titles the register has stopped, listed rather than deleted.

Titles the register has stopped.

Consolidated

Two titles absorbed into others

Where a proposed volume turned out to be a chapter of one that already existed, it was withdrawn and its host named. A separate volume would have added a title to the catalogue and nothing to the education.

Unsupported

Three the curriculum does not justify

Where a proposed resource had no basis in what the programme actually teaches, it was recorded as unsupported rather than built. Video support is the clearest case: producing it would be a decision about what the College is, not a gap in what it has written.

XIV Plainly

What the library is not, stated before what it is.

What it is not.

No licensed collection

WEC-LC holds no subscriptions to academic databases, no licensed journal access, no e-book collection and no interlibrary arrangement. A student enrolling here does not gain access to a research library. “Digital library access” on this site means the College’s own materials, and it is worth being exact about that rather than letting the phrase do work it has not earned.

XVI Reading Beyond The Programme

What the College recommends instead of what it does not hold.

What the College recommends instead.

A language learner needs far more input than any single programme supplies. Since the College cannot supply a collection, it says where the input actually is.

Free and vast

Public broadcasting and podcasts

Hours of natural speech at every level, at no cost, on any subject you already care about. Interest in the subject is what makes listening practice survive week three.

Free and vast

Public libraries and open collections

Graded readers, newspapers and open-access texts. A learner reading one thing a week they chose themselves will out-read the programme’s set texts many times over.

The one thing to avoid

Material far above your level

Input just beyond your current reach builds a language; input far beyond it builds nothing but discouragement. Your level page names where you are; choose accordingly.
