VI Citation and Use
How to cite, and what you may do with these

Cite as Worldwide English College, WEC Press, with the volume title and the edition year. You may download, print, photocopy and quote any volume here for teaching or study, including in another institution’s classroom. You may not present the College’s material as another body’s work, and you may not sell it. Nothing here has been reviewed by anyone who did not write it — On review says exactly what that means and offers a copy to anyone willing to change it.

The curriculum is open. The programme is the part you enrol in.
