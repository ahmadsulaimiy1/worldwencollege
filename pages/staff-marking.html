      How a mark is made here

      Against a published rubric

      The criteria were set before the work was, and they are in the task the learner was given. They are printed above every mark on this page so the standard being applied is the standard the learner was told about.

      With a reason the learner can read

      Feedback travels to the learner’s own record beside the mark. Write the one thing they should change; a mark with nothing behind it is a mark that cannot be acted on and cannot be appealed.

      And it can be looked at again

      Every mark is recorded against the account that gave it and the moment it was given. A learner who thinks a mark is wrong has a published route to say so, and that route reads this record. The academic regulations.
