        I The reference

        Why it is the only key, and what the College does when somebody loses one.

        One key, held by one person.

        No account, by design

        An account is created at enrolment rather than at application, so there is no password to forget between submitting a form and hearing back. The reference does the whole of the work in the meantime.

        An email address opens nothing

        This page has no field for one and the service behind it refuses one outright rather than ignoring it. Somebody who knows your name and your address, and not your reference, learns nothing here.

        Both halves of the answer

        Every outstanding item names whose it is. A step of the College’s is one you are entitled to chase, and this page tells you which ones those are rather than leaving “pending” to cover both.

        Placement within three working days

        The commitment the College makes to every applicant, published on the Admissions page and repeated here where it is owed. If it is missed, write to Admissions and say so.

        If you no longer have your reference

        Write to Admissions from the address you applied with and ask for it to be sent again. It is reissued to that address and to no other, which is the same rule stated from the other side: Contact the College.
