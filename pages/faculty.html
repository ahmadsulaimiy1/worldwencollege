Faculty

Instructors held to a professional standard, not a script.

The College's teaching is delivered by credentialed instructors, live and recorded, across all six levels of the IEFC, to a written method published in full on this site.

Teaching Philosophy

What every instructor teaches to, regardless of who they are.

Mapped, not improvised

Every class follows the IEFC's mapped curriculum. What one instructor teaches at a level is what every instructor teaches at that level.

Speaking from the first lesson

Guided speaking practice and live conversation are built into every level. Neither is an optional extra reached once the grammar is done.

Answerable to the record

Weekly quizzes, monthly assessments and continuous progress monitoring mean a teacher's judgement about a cohort can be checked against what the cohort actually did.

Appointment

The bar for joining the founding faculty.

Every instructor the College appoints is measured against the same standard, wherever in the world they teach from.

Certification & Fluency

  • A recognised English-teaching certification (CELTA, DELTA, TESOL, or equivalent) or a degree in English, Linguistics, or Education
  • Native or C2-level (CEFR Mastery) fluency in English

Experience & Fit

  • Demonstrated experience teaching to a structured, level-based curriculum — CEFR-aligned experience is a strong preference
  • Comfortable and effective teaching live, on video, in a fully online classroom
  • Alignment with the College's speaking-first, data-accountable teaching methodology (see Teaching Practice · The Method)
