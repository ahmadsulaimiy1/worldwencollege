# Fonts served from this directory

Four small files, all subsets, all supplying characters the display face
cannot draw. See the note at the head of `css/brand.css` for why each exists.

## Libre Bodoni — `librebodoni-latinext.woff2`, `librebodoni-latinext-italic.woff2`

Latin Extended subset, restricted in use to U+0100–017F, U+1E00–1E9F and
U+20A6. Supplies the macron letters (ā ī ū and capitals), the underdot set
(ḍ ḥ ṣ ṭ ẓ and capitals) and the naira sign, none of which are in Bodoni Moda.

- Family: Libre Bodoni, by Impallari Type
- Source: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Libre+Bodoni>

## EB Garamond — `ayn-hamza.woff2`, `ayn-hamza-italic.woff2`

A two-glyph subset — U+02BE ʾ and U+02BF ʿ — cut by the Google Fonts API.
1.2 KB each. Of eight faces measured, only EB Garamond and Inter carry ʿ.

- Family: EB Garamond, by Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo
- Source: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/EB+Garamond>
