Admissions

You are placed by assessment, never by self-declaration.

Five stages, each with a published time expectation. Assessment is free and comes before any fee is discussed — so you will know exactly what you are being offered, and what it costs, before you owe this College anything.

Request a Placement See the fees first
The five stages

From enquiry to your first ḥalaqah.

Enquire

Tell the Registry what you want to study and where you think you are. Nothing longer than a page, and no documents needed yet.

A day or two

Recite, or sit a placement

For the Qur’ān faculty: five minutes reciting to a teacher. For the others: a short written placement. Free, always — and it happens before any fee is discussed.

One sitting

Be placed

The Registry names your rung, your ḥalaqah, your teacher, your timetable and your tier. If we judge a track unrealistic for your schedule, we will say so rather than take the fee — and offer the track that is.

Within a week

Enrol

Sign the integrity and conduct undertaking, choose monthly, termly or yearly payment, and pay the first instalment.

Same day

Begin

Your ḥalaqah opens at the start of the next teaching week. Placement runs continuously — you are not asked to wait four months for a term to turn.

What you need

Entry requirements, by programme.

ProgrammeAcademic requirement Documents
Qur’ān faculty, all rungs None. Placement is by recitation and every rung from Q0 assumes nothing. ID at examination only
Arabic, L1–L3 None. Designed for absolute beginners with no Arabic at all. None to begin
Arabic, L4 and above Placement test, or completion of the rung below. None to begin
Ibtidā’iyyahNone. Minimum age 10. Two photographs; proof of age
I’dādiyyah Ibtidā’iyyah, or prior learning verified by examination. Ibtidā’iyyah certificate or study history; two photographs; ID
ThānawiyyahI’dādiyyah. Minimum age 15. I’dādiyyah certificate; two photographs; ID; purpose statement
Diploma — ‘Āliyah Thānawiyyah, or WAEC / NECO or a recognised equivalent. The certificate; two photographs; ID; two references
Ijāzah (Q5) Complete memorisation, and a reading assessed as clean by a chain-holder. Government ID
On documents you do not have

If you studied for years and hold nothing to show for it, that is the case this College was built for, not an obstacle to it. Tell the Registry where you read, with whom, and which books. We will verify what can be verified and examine you on the rest — and place you at the level your knowledge actually reaches, which may be several rungs above where a missing certificate would otherwise strand you.

Request a placement

Write to the Registry.

The College is opening its first cohort and admissions are handled personally by the Registry rather than through an application system. That is deliberate: a form cannot hear you recite.

What to include

  • Your name, your country, and the city you are writing from
  • Which faculty and which programme you are interested in
  • Where you think you currently are — honestly, not modestly
  • What you have studied before, with whom, and which books
  • How many hours a week you can genuinely give it
  • Whether you are applying for a kafālah place

You will have a reply within two working days. If you do not, write again — a College that does not answer its post has already failed the standard it published.
