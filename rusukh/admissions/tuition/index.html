Modes of tuition

Fees are set by mode of tuition, not by programme.

A student on any mode follows the same syllabus and sits the same examination. The mode determines the size of the class and the frequency of individual supervision.

Terms of payment

Payment, suspension and variation.

Instalments

Fees may be paid monthly, per semester or per session. Payment by semester or by session attracts a reduction, applied at the point of invoice. Monthly payment is due on the first working day of each month.

Suspension of studies

A student may suspend studies for up to two consecutive semesters and resume at the same point of the programme. No fee is charged during suspension and no fresh application is required. Application is made to the Registry.

Variation of mode

A student may move between the Class, Supervised and Individual modes at any monthly boundary. Fees are apportioned to the day.

Reductions

A reduction of twenty-five per cent applies to each member of a household after the first. A reduction of forty per cent applies to imāms and to teachers serving in recognised institutions.

Financial support

Supported places.

The College reserves a proportion of places in every cohort for students unable to meet the fees, and publishes the proportion achieved at the close of each session.

Administration of zakāh

Zakāh received by the College is held in a separate fund, applied solely to the fees of students falling within the eligible categories, and accounted for separately from tuition income. It is not applied to salaries, administration or the general purposes of the College.
