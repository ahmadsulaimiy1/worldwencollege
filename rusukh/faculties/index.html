Programmes of Study

Seventeen programmes across four faculties, leading to the Certificate, the Diploma, the Higher Diploma and, in the Faculty of the Qur’ān, to ijāzah. Each entry states the award, its normal duration, the mode of study and the conditions of entry.

Academic structure

Programmes are taught to prescribed texts and examined against published syllabi.

Every programme is composed of taught courses, each with a syllabus, a set of prescribed texts and a stated method of assessment. Syllabi are issued to students on matriculation and are available to applicants from the Registry on request.

The classical texts of the discipline are used as the teaching basis throughout. Where a programme requires a text to be committed to memory, this is stated in the syllabus and forms part of the oral examination.

Classes are capped at twelve students. Instruction is by scheduled live class, with sessions recorded and made available to enrolled students for revision.

Prescribed texts

Tuḥfat al-Aṭfāl and al-Muqaddimah al-Jazariyyah in the Diploma in Tajwīd; al-Shāṭibiyyah and al-Durrah in the Higher Diploma in the Ten Readings, with Ṭayyibat al-Nashr in the extended route. Texts marked in the syllabus as maḥfūẓ are examined orally from memory.

Prescribed texts

Al-‘Arabiyyah bayna Yadayk in the certificate levels; al-Ājurrūmiyyah and al-Binā’ wa’l-Asās in the Diploma; Qaṭr al-Nadā, Shadhā al-‘Arf, al-Balāghah al-Wāḍiḥah and Alfiyyat Ibn Mālik in the Higher Diploma.

The Intensive Arabic Programme runs over three months in three stages — grammar, morphology and rhetoric — with classes on four evenings a week and a fifth day given to assignment review. Class times are published in West Africa Time (UTC+1).

Teaching basis

Instruction in jurisprudence follows the Mālikī school, which is that of the majority of the students the College serves. Provision for the Shāfi‘ī and Ḥanafī schools will be announced when the faculty for each has been appointed. Doctrine is taught according to Ahl al-Sunnah wa’l-Jamā‘ah.

The Diploma in Qur’ānic Pedagogy prepares graduates of the Faculty of the Qur’ān to conduct classes, correct recitation and examine to the College’s standard. It is the College’s principal means of extending its teaching capacity, and is offered without fee to serving teachers in recognised institutions.
