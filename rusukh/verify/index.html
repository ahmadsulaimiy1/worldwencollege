Registry

Verification of Instruments

Certificates, transcripts and instruments of ijāzah issued by the College may be verified against their reference number by any party, without charge and without reference to the holder.

Procedure

Verification by reference number.

Every instrument issued by the College bears a reference number in the form DAR-«year»-«award»-«serial». On receipt of that number the Registry confirms:

  • whether the instrument is genuine and in force;
  • the name of the holder as recorded in the register;
  • the award, the date of conferral and the examining body;
  • in the case of an ijāzah, the chain of transmission as stated on the instrument;
  • whether the award has been revoked, and the date of revocation.
Enquiries

registry@daralrusukh.com
Quoting the reference number.

Answered within two working days.

Verification is conducted by the Registry. A self-service register will be published as the number of instruments in issue warrants it.

Awards not issued by the College

The College confers the Certificate, the Diploma, the Higher Diploma, the graded certificates of Islamic education and ijāzah bi-sanad muttaṣil. It confers no degree. Any instrument presented as a degree of this College should be reported to the Registry.
