Recognition of prior study

Candidates who have completed a course of study at a madrasah or other institution without receiving formal certification may be admitted directly to examination at the I’dādiyyah or Thānawiyyah stage. The Registry assesses such applications on a documented account of where, with whom and in which texts the candidate studied, together with the full written and oral examination for the stage sought. Admission under this provision confers no exemption from examination.

Examination

Regulations of examination.

Standard

The pass mark is seventy per cent. Where a programme is examined by both written paper and oral viva, each must be passed independently; a mark in one does not compensate for a failure in the other.

Oral examination

An oral examination is required for every award of the College without exception, and is conducted by a member of faculty other than the candidate’s own teacher.

Identification

Candidates present a government-issued identity document before the final examination of any programme. Proof of age is required at registration for the graded certificates.

Conduct of examinations

Examinations are conducted remotely under live supervision. The Examinations Office assigns candidates to sittings and issues the timetable in advance of each semester’s examinations.

Re-examination

A candidate who fails may be re-examined at the following sitting. The number of permitted re-examinations is set in the regulations of each programme and issued with the syllabus.

Issue of instruments

Certificates are issued within seven working days of the confirmation of results, in digital form with the transcript, and in printed form on request. The mode of study is recorded on the instrument.

All examination is overseen by the Academic Council (المجلس العلمي), which approves the syllabi, appoints examiners and confirms results.

Ijāzah

Conditions governing the grant of ijāzah.

Ijāzah bi-sanad muttaṣil is a licence to transmit, carrying a chain of transmission from the conferring teacher to the Prophet ﷺ. The following conditions govern its grant by this College.

Authority to confer

Ijāzah is conferred only by a member of faculty who himself holds a connected chain in the narration being conferred.

Regulation 1

Statement of the chain

The instrument states the chain of transmission in full, naming each transmitter in order, together with the narration conferred and the date and place of the final recitation.

Regulation 2

Complete recitation

The candidate recites the Qur’ān in its entirety to the conferring teacher, conducted live. Recordings are not accepted in fulfilment of this condition.

Regulation 3

Verification

Every instrument bears a reference number against which it may be verified by any party, without charge and without reference to the holder.

Regulation 4

Register of Asānīd

The chains held by the faculty, the transmitters through whom they came, and the members of faculty authorised to confer each, are entered in the Register of Asānīd and published.

Academic conduct

The academic undertaking.

Every student signs an undertaking at matriculation covering honesty in examination, the accuracy of documents submitted to the Registry, and proper conduct toward faculty and fellow students. Members of faculty are bound by the same undertaking.

Breach is a matter for the Academic Council. Where an award has been obtained by misrepresentation, the Council may revoke it and record the revocation against the reference number, which the verification service will report.
