Admission to the Higher Diploma in the Ten Readings

The Academic Council does not open a programme in which a candidate would be examined for a chain by the sole member of faculty competent to confer it. The Higher Diploma in the Ten Readings therefore opens upon the appointment of a second holder, and the vacancy is entered in this register until it is filled.

Form of the instrument

An instrument of ijāzah issued by the College states the narration conferred; the chain of transmission in full, naming each transmitter in order from the conferring teacher to the Prophet ﷺ; the date and place of the final recitation; and the reference number against which the instrument may be verified.
