The College

Instruction at a distance, to the standard of a resident faculty.

Dār al-Rusūkh admits students to structured programmes of study in the Qur’ānic and Islamic sciences. Teaching is conducted in small supervised classes by appointed faculty, assessment is by written and oral examination, and awards are conferred by the Academic Council on the recommendation of the examining board.

The College maintains no campus. Instruction is delivered by scheduled live class throughout, in the manner of the affiliated and external programmes long established at Al-Azhar and the University of London. Students are admitted to a named programme, placed at a level determined by assessment, and progress through published syllabi under a faculty member responsible for their record.

Languages of instruction are Arabic and English. The academic year comprises three semesters, kept against the Hijrī calendar.

Faculties

Four faculties.

Each faculty admits to its own programmes and maintains its own syllabi and examinations under the Academic Council.

Faculty I كلية القرآن الكريم وعلومه

The Qur’ān and its Sciences

Recitation, Tajwīd, memorisation, and the readings. Programmes range from a foundation certificate for beginners to the Higher Diploma in the Ten Readings.

6 programmes Faculty II كلية اللغة العربية

Arabic Language

Arabic for speakers of other languages, and the grammatical and rhetorical sciences, to Diploma and Advanced Diploma level.

4 programmes Faculty III كلية الشريعة

Sharī’ah Sciences

Creed, jurisprudence and its principles, the sciences of ḥadīth, exegesis and Prophetic biography.

4 programmes Faculty IV كلية الدعوة والدراسات التطبيقية

Da’wah and Applied Studies

Islamic communication in English, translation of Islamic texts, the formation of teachers, and professional studies.

The College additionally examines candidates for the graded certificates of general Islamic education — Ibtidā’iyyah, I’dādiyyah and Thānawiyyah — including candidates who have completed their studies elsewhere and hold no formal certification. Applications under this provision are assessed by the Registry on the evidence of prior study together with full examination.

Academic awards and regulations
Admission

Admission is by application and assessment.

Application

The applicant submits an application to the Registry naming the faculty and programme sought, with a record of prior study.

Two working days

Assessment

Applicants to the Faculty of the Qur’ān are assessed by recitation before a member of faculty. Other applicants sit a written placement paper. No charge is made for assessment.

One sitting

Placement

The Registry issues an offer naming the programme, the level of entry, the class and the supervising member of faculty.

Within one week

Matriculation

The student signs the academic undertaking, settles the first instalment of fees, and is entered on the register.

On acceptance

Commencement

Classes begin at the start of the following teaching week. Admission is continuous and does not wait upon the semester.
