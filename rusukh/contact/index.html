Every office of this College, by name.

Write to the office that handles what you need and you will have a reply within two working days. If you do not, write again — a College that does not answer its post has already failed the standard it published.

Monday to Thursday 09:00–17:00 · Friday 09:00–12:00 · Saturday and Sunday closed.
All times West Africa Time (UTC+1), which does not change through the year.

The College keeps an administrative registry in Lagos, Nigeria. It operates no campus and makes no claim to one — all teaching is delivered at a distance by design. A full postal address is issued to enrolled students and to anyone who needs to send documents; write to the Registry and it will be given to you.

Every email address on this page is on the College’s own domain. That is a deliberate correction: the institution that preceded this one published five addresses across four domains, none of which was its own website’s, and two of which were on a top-level domain a Nigerian college cannot register.
