Continuing Education

Short Courses

Courses of four to eight weeks, examined and certificated, admitting a new cohort at the beginning of each month. Each course corresponds to a component of a degree-programme syllabus, and completion is credited toward the corresponding programme on subsequent admission.

Ramaḍān

The Ramaḍān programme.

The general timetable of the College is suspended for the month, and the following programme is offered in its place.

Accelerated memorisation

Daily classes at increased portion for students already admitted to the Diploma in Qur’ānic Memorisation.

Khatm circles

Completion of the Qur’ān within the month, recited in class under correction.

Nightly exegesis

A daily half-hour upon the portion appointed for that night’s reading.

Preparation for Tarāwīḥ

For those appointed to lead, in the fortnight preceding the month.

Memorisation schedules

Schedules of memorisation.

The Faculty of the Qur’ān issues four graduated schedules for the memorisation of the Qur’ān, each incorporating a programme of revision from the first week. They are supplied without charge, and without condition of enrolment.

Fourteen months

Two to three hours daily.

Ten months

Presupposes a sound reading and a cleared timetable.

Six months

For those already holding a substantial portion.

Five months

Full-time study.
