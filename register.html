The Graduate Register

The College's roll of award holders. Every award of the International English Fluency Course appears here — from English Aspirant to English Laureate — because each level is a complete achievement and not a waypoint.

Graduates appear here only with their consent. An award that is not listed is not thereby unverified — every award can be checked by its code at Award Verification.

    About this register

    Every award, at every level

    The International English Fluency Course confers an award at each of its six levels. All of them are recorded here. A register that listed only its highest graduates would say by omission that the other awards were steps rather than achievements.

    Listed by consent

    No graduate is published without agreeing to be. Consent governs this page alone: it does not govern verification, because a verification code is something a graduate chose to hand someone, and honouring it is the entire purpose.

    Every entry is checkable

    Each listing carries its verification code and links to its own record. A register of names that could not be checked would be a claim on a webpage; the College's position is that its public claims are verifiable, and that has to include this one.

    Withdrawn awards are not listed here

    An award that has been withdrawn or superseded leaves this roll but is never deleted from the Register. Its verification page continues to resolve and states its standing, with the date. Verification, not this page, is the authoritative answer.
