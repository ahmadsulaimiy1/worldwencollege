What you will meet.

This section describes the parts of the College a student deals with directly — how you are taught, how you are assessed, what is recorded about you, and what you can do about any of it.

The Student Portal Academic Regulations
On Day One

What is waiting when you enrol.

All of it is available immediately. Nothing unlocks on a date, because there are no dates — see Dates.

Your level

Ten modules

The level you were placed into, with its modules, lessons, exercises and assessments already written and waiting. Nothing is drip-fed.

The Listening Lab

Recording and pronunciation work

Where you record yourself against pronunciation targets and where those recordings are kept, so that six months of change is audible rather than assumed.

Your record

Your marks, held by you

Every attempt, every mark and every piece of feedback, visible to you as it accumulates — not summarised at the end of the level.

The rules

Published before assessment

Criteria, thresholds and outcomes are published before you are assessed against them. A standard revealed afterwards is not a standard.
